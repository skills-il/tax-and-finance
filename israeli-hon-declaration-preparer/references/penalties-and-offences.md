# Penalties and offences attaching to a capital declaration

Source: the Income Tax Ordinance as published by gov.il, and the warning box on form 1219.

## The form's own warning

Failing to file on time, together with all the required documents, attracts a fine for each month
of delay under section 188(z) and constitutes an offence under section 215. Recording false
particulars in the declaration, omitting assets in your ownership, and failing to file on time are
addressed by sections 216 and 220.

## The fine, and why no current shekel figure appears here

- **Section 188(z)** imposes a fine per month of delay on a report under section 135(1). The
  Ordinance names 200 shekels per month, and a higher figure of 400 shekels where the filer
  obtained a later filing date at their own request and then filed after that later date, running
  from the later date. Note the 400 is an **increase**, not a reduction.
- **Section 188(e)** defines "month" in that section as a full month.
- **Section 188(h)** re-indexes every amount stated in section 188 on 1 January of each tax year, to
  the index of the preceding year.

Because of 188(h), **neither nominal figure is a current amount**. State the mechanism and route
the user to the Tax Authority for the figure in force. Do not quote an indexed number.

A separate fixed administrative fine attaches to a section 216 offence under the Administrative
Offences (Administrative Fine, Tax Enactments) Regulations 5747-1987, and those regulations double
the fine on a repeat offence and triple it on a further repeat. No amount is stated here: the
regulations were not read as a primary source during authoring, and secondary sources gave
irreconcilable figures. Any single number would be incomplete without the escalation rule in any
case.

## Fine and prosecution do not stack

**Section 189(b):** once a criminal charge is brought for failing to file, no fine under section 188
is payable for that offence, and a fine already paid is refunded. On acquittal, linkage and interest
run from the date of payment to the date of refund.

## The offences

| Section | Offence | Penalty |
|---|---|---|
| 215 | Contravening the Ordinance or a regulation under it, where no other express penalty is prescribed | One year, or a fine under section 61(a)(2) |
| 216(1) | Without sufficient reason, failing to comply with a requirement contained in a notice given under the Ordinance | One year, or a fine under section 61(a)(2) |
| 220 | Wilfully, intending to evade tax, omitting income from a report or making a false statement or entry | Seven years, or a fine under section 61(a)(4) plus twice the concealed income |

## Preparer liability

- **Section 143:** a person who assists another for payment in preparing a report, notice, form or
  other document for the purposes of the Ordinance must declare on that document that they assisted.
- **Section 224:** a person who assisted another to prepare a report, notice or other document
  knowing it contains information that is untrue is treated as if they had themselves committed the
  offences under sections 215 to 217 and 220.

This is why an AI tool cannot occupy the paid-preparer role. The block is for a human who assisted
for payment and who carries personal liability for what the document contains.

## Burden of proof on appeal

**Section 155:** the burden of proving that an assessment is excessive lies on the appellant.
It shifts to the assessing officer or the Director only where the appellant kept acceptable books.
This is the reason the capital comparison and its explanations are assembled before filing.
