# Changelog

## 1.3.0 - 2026-08-27

Corrected four destination descriptions that sent users to the wrong place. govcarins.mof.gov.il is a comprehensive-insurance simulator for state employees priced off the annual state tender, not a general educational tool. The car.cma.gov.il troubleshooting entry described a licence-plate field and a registration wait that do not exist on that tool. The no-claims (shin-nun) record was attributed to HaPool, which is the residual-market insurer rather than a claims database; corrected to Har HaBituach, the insurer's own certificate, and INFOCAR. Maccabi's supplementary ladder has three tiers, with Kesef as the entry tier, not two with Zahav as the basic.

Added HaPool as the route for drivers no insurer will take, two-wheeler mandatory insurance, the CMA Service Index as a comparison input alongside price and coverage, the permitted-driver check on the mandatory certificate, and a Reference Links section. Recorded the August 2026 Clalit-Ayalon long-term-care agreement.

Removed several figures that no source supported, including a per-sqm rebuild rate that risked understating cover, and replaced them with instructions to obtain the real number.

## 1.2.3 - 2026-08-13

Repaired the dead Kol-Zchut SHABAN citation. The claim that the Ministry of Health sets SHABAN premiums is not supported by the source and was replaced with what the source does state: pricing is uniform for everyone in the same age band and plan within a kupa.
