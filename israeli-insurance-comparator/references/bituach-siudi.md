## Bituach Siudi (Long-Term Care) Crisis 2025-2026

The Israeli long-term-care market is in a multi-year transition, and any 2026 comparison must surface it. Be careful with the dates: the original deadlines were a Ministry of Health draft directive that was NOT enforced.

- **The proposed order making the kupot stop selling new bituach siudi from July 1, 2025 was never finalized.** That date passed with no enforcement and kupot-channel sales continued. The operative horizon moved back: the kupot-channel handoff runs to **end of 2026**, and the CMA's removal of the "self-liquidation/run-off" clause that lets insurers exit takes effect in 2027. The policies are being wound down, but on a 2026-2027 timeline, not the original 2025 dates.
- **The CMA tightened patient eligibility in its December 2024 long-term-care regulations:** a siudi patient is someone unable to perform four of the six ADLs (activities of daily living), or three as long as sphincter control is one of them. Previously any three sufficed, so some patients who qualified under the old definition no longer do.
- **Clalit's policy has an operator again (August 2026).** Harel withdrew in 2024, the following tender drew no bidders, and Harel agreed to serve until end-2026. On 16 August 2026 Clalit announced it had signed with Ayalon to insure its members **from January 2027 for eight years**. The underlying longevity risk is not resolved, and the other kupot must be checked separately rather than assumed to be in the same position.
- **Options for new coverage:** existing kupot-channel policies (still in force during the transition), private bituach siudi from the major insurers, and Bituach Leumi's siudi track. Pricing and underwriting differ significantly; verify current availability per insurer.

When a user asks about "bituach siudi", surface this transition, but do NOT state the July-2025 / January-2026 dates as accomplished facts.
