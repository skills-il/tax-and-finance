# Israeli Utility & Telecom Details (2026)

Detailed rate tables, provider lists, saving tips, and extended troubleshooting moved out of SKILL.md to keep the main file lean. Rates reset periodically (electricity now semi-annually, water/arnona annually, telecom continuously); always verify live at the source before quoting.

## Electricity rate snapshot (2026, incl. 18% VAT)

- Standard residential tariff: ~0.6352 ILS/kWh (63.52 agorot inc. 18% VAT; 53.83 agorot before VAT), in force from 1.7.2026 after a cut of about 0.57%. The January 2026 edition was 64.32 agorot (+1.5% over 2025) and is superseded. The Electricity Authority updates the tariff on a semi-annual cycle, so a rate can shift mid-year; verify before quoting.
- TOU (תעו"ז), 2-tier since Apr 2023, THREE seasons, in force 1.7.2026 (incl. 18% VAT): summer (Jun-Sep) peak 17:00-23:00 = 172.23 (145.96 excl.), off-peak 50.42; winter (Dec-Feb) peak 17:00-22:00 = 114.70, off-peak 46.63; transition (Mar-May, Oct-Nov) peak 49.58, off-peak 45.78. The winter peak band applies on weekdays, Fri/eves AND Shabbat/holidays. There is no 06:00-08:00 morning band. Source: https://www.iec.co.il/content/tariffs/contentpages/taozb-namuch
- Fixed line items: connection fee, distribution fee, public-broadcasting fee. Apartments also pay a share of common-area electricity (חשמל כללי) via the va'ad bayit, separate from the IEC bill.

## Private electricity suppliers (residential)

As of 2026, roughly 360,000 customers have moved to private suppliers (up from ~280,000 at end-2025); a 520 MW private-generation tender from Feb 2026 is accelerating the shift. Typical discount 5-21% on the **generation** component only (~60-70% of the bill); the household still pays IEC for distribution, transmission, broadcasting, and metering. 14-day cooling-off under the Consumer Protection Law (the Energy Ministry is working to shorten switching to 7 days in 2026). Active suppliers include Cellcom Energy, Partner, Bezeq, HOT Energy, Pazgas Electricity, Amisragas, Electra Power, OPC Energy, Dalia Energy, Enlight.

## Electricity bill discounts (separate from supplier choice)

- Seniors with income supplement (השלמת הכנסה): 50% on up to 400 kWh/month (800 per bi-monthly bill). A plan to deepen this to 65% (old-age + income-supplement, old-age-disability, Holocaust survivors) is advancing, verify at gov.il before quoting 65%.
- Holocaust survivors: 50% on up to 400 kWh/month.
- Disabled with high disability %: tiers per Bituach Leumi recognition.
- Applied automatically once Bituach Leumi shares eligibility with IEC; verify the discount line appears on the bill.

## Electricity debt, disconnection, and protected-consumer status

- Arrears: request a הסדר תשלומים (payment arrangement) from IEC/the supplier before disconnection.
- Disconnection for non-payment is regulated by the Electricity Authority אמות מידה: advance written notice, a minimum-debt threshold, and no disconnection while a billing dispute is open.
- צרכן מוגן (protected consumer): households dependent on electrically-powered life-support / medical equipment register with IEC and cannot be disconnected; they also get restoration priority during outages.

## Solar net-metering ROI detail

- **Roof-control gate (check FIRST):** only someone who controls the roof/connection point can install. Apartment residents need רכוש-משותף consent (building owners' agreement / roof-rights allocation under חוק המקרקעין / התקנון המצוי); renters generally cannot install at all. A shared-roof / roof-lease arrangement is the alternative for apartments.
- System cost 2026: ~3,500-5,500 ILS/kWp turnkey (small systems cost more per kWp). Typical 5 kWp home system ~18,000-22,000 ILS, consistent with the per-kWp band above.
- Annual production: ~1,500-1,800 kWh/kWp (Negev ~1,800, north ~1,400-1,500, central ~1,600).
- Metering: it is a מונה נטו (net-metering) credit via a bidirectional meter (מונה דו-כיווני), kWh are credited by netting, not a meter physically spinning backward.
- Export tariff regime (2025 two-track, adopted): residential rooftop export ~48 agorot/kWh, plus a +6 agorot "urban premium" (cities >50k) for the first 15 years (up to ~54 agorot). The small-installation track is ≤15 kW (a tiny self-consumption track ≤8 kW). The old energy-vs-energy net-metering is CLOSED to new entrants; the biggest saving is now self-consumption (avoids the full ~63.5 agorot retail rate). Confirm the exact track on the installer's quote.
- Payback typically 4-7 years (faster in the south); lifetime 25+ years, ~0.5%/yr degradation.

## Water tariff snapshot (2026, incl. VAT + sewage)

- Ascending block: Tier 1 (כמות מוכרת) ~8.508 ILS/m³; Tier 2 ~15.623 ILS/m³ (~84% higher). Tier-1 allocation ~3.5 m³ per registered nefesh/month.
- Residential water is purely volumetric, there is NO fixed household water service charge; sewage is bundled into the per-m³ tariff in most municipalities. Where billed separately, standalone sewer ~4.39 ILS/m³.
- Register all nefashot to maximize the Tier-1 allocation. Confirmed-leak credit (often 50-100%) under תקנות תאגידי מים וביוב with a plumber certificate filed within 60 days.
- Municipal corporations: Mei Avivim (Tel Aviv), Hagihon (Jerusalem), Mei Haifa, Mei Raanana, Mekorot (some localities).

## Cooking gas and natural gas

- LPG 12 kg balloon: price regulated by the Energy Ministry, max published monthly at gov.il; delivery fee varies. Israeli LPG suppliers: Supergas, Pazgas, Amisragas, Dorgas.
- **Central gas (גז מרכזי / צובר):** apartments on a shared bulk tank are served by ONE LPG supplier, but the household has the RIGHT to switch central-gas supplier and to demand the supplier's published price list (LPG reform under חוק הפיקוח על מצרכים ושירותים). Switching supplier or negotiating the price is the main lever for these households, see Kol-Zchut "החלפת ספק גז לצרכן ביתי".
- Natural gas (גז טבעי): available in newer buildings on the grid; ~40-60% cheaper per cooking hour but with a monthly connection fee. Supply operator: Energean Israel (Karish/Tanin field), a separate company from NewMed Energy (formerly Delek Drilling, Tamar/Leviathan); last-mile via local distribution companies.

## Home internet (2026)

Separate infrastructure (תשתית) and ISP (ספק) layers; most households now buy a single wholesale-market bundle ("שוק סיטונאי").

| Infrastructure operator | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Bezeq (BFiber) | National FTTH (2021-2025) |
| HOT | National, cable + fiber |
| IBC / Unlimited | ~260 localities, symmetric 100Mbps-5Gbps |
| Cellcom + Partner self-built fiber | Selected cities |

| Speed | Typical 2026 bundled price (NIS/month) |
|---|---|
| 100Mbps | 70-90 |
| 300Mbps | 90-110 |
| 600Mbps | 100-130 |
| 1Gbps | 99-160 (HOT-Net ~99, BFiber ~149) |
| 2.5-5Gbps | 180-300+ |

Consolidating a legacy split (infrastructure + ISP) into a wholesale bundle usually drops 20-40 NIS/month. ADSL on copper is being phased out; fiber reaches >90% of households, the upgrade is typically free with a new contract. Drop router rental (10-20 NIS/month) by owning a compatible ONT/router. Triple-play (cellular+internet+TV) discounts vanish if you cancel one leg, unbundling + a streaming service is usually cheaper unless you want TV.

## Arnona discounts and reliefs

- Olim: up to 90% on the first 100 sqm for 12 of the 24 months after aliyah registration (single benefit, not yearly). Disabled/sicud-recognized olim get up to 80% with no time limit.
- Seniors: eligibility at the (rising) retirement age, women's age is climbing in steps toward 65 under חוק גיל פרישה, so do not quote a flat "62"; men 67. 30% if income is below the average wage (~13,623 ILS single, Jan 2026); up to 100% with income supplement.
- Low income (sliding scale via municipal welfare), disabled (80% for 90%+), IDF/bereaved families, students, single-person (municipality-dependent).
- **Empty-property relief (הנחת נכס ריק):** a vacant, unused property gets an exemption for a limited period (typically up to 6 months, once per ownership) under תקנות הסדרים במשק המדינה (הנחה מארנונה) התשנ"ג-1993.
- **Uninhabitable property (נכס שאינו ראוי לשימוש):** an exemption for a property unfit for use / under major renovation, under פקודת העיריות §330.
- Only ONE discount tier applies per property (the larger, not the sum).

## Bill-reduction tips

**Electricity:** switch to TOU if you have a smart meter; run washer/dryer/dishwasher off-peak (after 23:00 in summer, after 22:00 in winter; Shabbat is NOT off-peak in winter, when the 17:00-22:00 peak band applies on Shabbat and holidays too); LED lighting; AC at 24-25°C with ceiling fans; maintain the solar water heater (dud shemesh); unplug standby loads (5-10%).

**Water:** register all nefashot; fix leaking toilets/faucets fast (a running toilet wastes 200+ L/day); low-flow showerheads and aerators; dual-flush; irrigate in the evening.

**Arnona:** verify recorded size matches measured size; apply for every eligible discount; pay annually for a 1-2% discount where offered; report household-composition changes; if you run a business from home, confirm you are not charged commercial rates on the whole property.

**Cellular & internet:** compare against MVNOs (most users overpay 30-60 NIS/month); ask for "מחיר שמירת לקוח" (retention, 15-30% off); reminder for the month-12 promo expiry; unbundle triple-play if you skip TV; port for free; own your router.

## Extended troubleshooting

**High electricity bill vs consumption:** check whether the bill shows an actual reading (קריאת מונה) or estimate (הערכה), request an actual reading if estimated; compare the physical meter; check the electric water heater timer, AC filter cleanliness (dirty filters +15-20%), old fridge (15+ yrs uses ~30% more), phantom loads; apartments also pay common-area electricity. Use the IEC app to spot spikes.

**High water bill:** most often a leak, add food coloring to the toilet tank and wait 15 min without flushing; read the meter before bed and again in the morning with no water use; verify nefashot are registered (unregistered members push consumption into Tier 2); request a meter accuracy test; a confirmed hidden leak may qualify for a bill adjustment.

**Can't find the TOU option:** TOU needs a smart meter; call IEC 103 (or SMS/WhatsApp 055-7000103) to check eligibility and request installation (free from IEC; self-paid expedited ~260 ILS where not yet deployed). TOU only helps if you can shift consumption to off-peak.

**Cellular bill jumped after the promo:** no-commitment plans have no exit fee, sign with a new provider (Rami Levy, Golan, 019, or a retention deal) and let them handle portability in ~1 day; do not cancel first. If on an "התחייבות" plan, check the early-exit fee, switching is often still cheaper.

**Consolidating split Bezeq + ISP bills:** pick a wholesale bundle from any infrastructure operator or a reselling ISP; the new provider handles cancellation and the ONT/router swap; expect a 20-40 NIS/month drop.

## Escalation and complaint channels

- Arnona: file a השגה (objection) within 90 days → the municipality replies within 60 days → appeal to ועדת ערר לארנונה within 30 days → בית משפט לעניינים מנהליים within 45 days.
- Before court, use each regulator's public-complaints unit (פניות ציבור of רשות החשמל / רשות המים / רשות הגז, the משרד התקשורת complaints unit) and the הרשות להגנת הצרכן ולסחר הוגן.
- Utility billing disputes (not arnona) can go to small-claims court (בית משפט לתביעות קטנות), cap 39,900 ILS in 2026.

- Low income (hakhnasot nemukhot): significant discounts on a sliding scale, apply through the municipal welfare department
- Disabled (nekhim): discounts based on disability percentage; 80% for 90%+ disability
- National service / IDF veterans: various discounts, including bereaved families
- Students: some municipalities offer student discounts
- Single-person household: some municipalities offer discounts

## Arnona objection procedure (moved from SKILL.md, 2026-08-01)

**Filing an arnona objection (hassagah):** If you believe the bill is wrong (incorrect property size, wrong zone classification, missing discount, balcony charged at full rate), there is a formal escalation path:
1. **Hassagah**, file a written objection with the municipal arnona manager within **90 days** of receiving the bill. Cite the specific calculation error and attach evidence (measurement, classification documents, photos of enclosed vs open balcony).
2. **Response**, the municipality must respond within 60 days. Silence after 60 days is treated as deemed-rejection and the clock for the next step starts.
3. **Appeal to ועדת ערר**, if rejected, you have **30 days** to appeal to the local arnona objection committee (ועדת ערר לארנונה).
4. **Administrative court**, if the ועדת ערר ruling is against you, escalate to בית משפט לעניינים מנהליים within 45 days of the decision.
