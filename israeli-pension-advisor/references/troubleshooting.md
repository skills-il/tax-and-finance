# Troubleshooting

### Error: "Pension fund not transferring"
Cause: Switching pension funds requires a specific signed transfer request to the receiving fund.
Solution: Contact the new fund to initiate. Send the request to the old fund only after deposits to the new fund have begun. The transferring fund must complete the transfer within 10 business days of a complete signed request (2008 Transfer Regulations, reg. 5(a)); the Commissioner may extend by up to 20 more business days. No fees. If transferring from pre-2013 bituach menahalim, verify whether you forfeit the guaranteed annuity factor before signing.

### Error: "Employer not contributing"
Cause: Employer legally required to contribute pension after 6 months (no prior pension) or 3 months retroactive (with prior pension)
Solution: Employer must contribute retroactively from the applicable start date. Contact Ministry of Labor (Misrad HaAvoda) or the pension fund for enforcement.

### Error: "Cannot withdraw keren hishtalmut"
Cause: Lock-in period not completed
Solution: Standard lock-in is 6 years from first deposit; 3 years for accredited training with documentation, or once an employee has reached retirement age. For an employee, withdrawal before maturity taxes the employer's deposits AND the gains at the marginal rate; the employee's own deposits come back untaxed.

### Error: "I withdrew severance and now regret it -- can I restore rezef-pitzuyim?"
Cause: Severance was paid out (either by explicit Part B election, or by following a pre-2024 procedure that defaulted to payout). The rezef-pitzuyim option has lapsed.
Solution: Form 161ג reverts a rezef election; it is not a route for returning severance already withdrawn. This skill has no verified procedure for undoing a payout, so do not promise one: send the user to the Tax Authority (pakid shuma) or a licensed pension adviser.
