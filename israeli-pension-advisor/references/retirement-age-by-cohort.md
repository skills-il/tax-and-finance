# Retirement age for women, by year of birth

Source: https://www.gov.il/he/pages/women_retirement_age_news
Retirement age for women by year of birth (Retirement Age Law 5764-2004 as
amended, in force from January 2022; the increase is phased over 11 years):

| Year of birth | Retirement age | Year of birth | Retirement age |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1960 | 62 + 4 months | 1966 | 64 |
| 1961 | 62 + 8 months | 1967 | 64 + 3 months |
| 1962 | 63 | 1968 | 64 + 6 months |
| 1963 | 63 + 3 months | 1969 | 64 + 9 months |
| 1964 | 63 + 6 months | 1970 and later | 65 |
| 1965 | 63 + 9 months | | |

The age is set by DATE of birth, not by calendar year, and not by a flat
number. The Retirement Age Law 5764-2004 as amended came into force in January
2022 and phases the increase from 62 to 65 over 11 years. Men's retirement age
is 67. Mandatory retirement age (gil prishah chovah) is 67 for both genders and
is a separate concept from pension eligibility age.
