# Approaching retirement

Detail for users within about five years of retirement. Every rule below is
general information, not pension advice; the fund and the Tax Authority have
the final word on any figure.

## Bituach Leumi old-age pension (kitzvat zikna)

- **Two ages, not one.** A woman is entitled from her retirement age (62 and 4
  months to 65, by year of birth, see `retirement-age-by-cohort.md`) subject to
  an income test; from age 70 she is entitled with no income test. Men: 67 with
  the income test, 70 without it. A qualifying insurance period is also required.
  (National Insurance Law, s.245 and Table A1.)
- **Pensions do not count in the income test.** Pensions paid under law, a
  collective agreement or an employment contract are excluded from "income" for
  this test (s.245(c)). Above the maximum income the
  pension is not cut to zero at once: it is reduced by 60% of the excess, and
  never below 10% of the pension (s.245(b)).
- **Deferral increment:** the pension grows by 5% for each year between
  retirement age and 70 in which it was not paid because work income exceeded
  the test, or in which the insured waived it (s.249).
- **It stacks.** The BL pension is paid on top of a private pension; it is not
  an offset. It does NOT count toward the minimum pension that the Tikun 190
  lump-sum route and pension commutation require.

## Unemployment benefit for women born 1960 or later

A woman born on or after 1 January 1960 who has turned 57 is entitled to up to
300 days of unemployment benefit, counted over 18 months, instead of the usual
maximum (National Insurance Law s.171(a)(1b)-(1c)). Relevant for anyone laid off
in the years before the raised retirement age.

## Transition grant for women born 1960 to 1966 (s.269A)

Added with the 2021 increase in women's retirement age. It is paid for the four
months after the woman turns 62, and only if, among other conditions, she has
no work income, her pension income does not exceed 73.5% of the average wage,
her other income in the previous tax year did not exceed 60,000 NIS, and she
receives no unemployment, disability or income-support benefit. The statute
lists monthly amounts from 4,000 NIS (born 1960 to 1962) down to 3,000 NIS
(born 1966); confirm the current amount with Bituach Leumi before quoting it.
The claim window is up to 24 months.

## Commutation (hivun) of pension savings

- The tagmulim part of a pension fund cannot be cashed out freely. A lump sum
  comes from commuting part of the pension (Gemel Law s.23).
- Commutation is unlimited as long as the pension left afterwards, together
  with pensions from other kitzbah funds and budget pensions, is at least the
  minimum pension: 5,306 NIS/month in 2026. Bituach Leumi does not count.
- If the remaining pension would be at or below that minimum, commutation is
  limited to up to 25% of the pension from that fund, for up to 5 years.
- Tax: a commuted qualifying pension can be exempt, but the exempt amount is
  taken out of the lifetime exemption pool (976,005 NIS for a 2026 retiree) and
  reduces the monthly exemption later. The exemption must be requested on the
  Director's form within 90 days of the first time a pension exemption was
  granted (Income Tax Ordinance s.9A(e)(1)); a missed deadline loses it. A commuted recognised pension (Tikun
  190 money) is taxed 15% on the gain.

## Fixation of rights (kibua zechuyot, Form 161ד)

Form 161ד is the Tax Authority's "request for fixation of rights under Section
9A". At retirement it allocates the lifetime exemption pool between the monthly
pension exemption and capital or severance grants, after deducting severance
already withdrawn during the career under heichum kitzbah (1.35 per shekel).
It is often the largest single tax decision at retirement, so it belongs with a
licensed pension adviser or a CPA. The Tax Authority's 2026 filling-out guide
is linked in `evidence.json`.

## Public-sector budget pension (pensia taktziveet)

Employees on a budget pension are paid by the employer rather than from an
accumulated fund balance, so the fund-based ceilings and commutation rules
above do not map onto them directly. Ask whether the user
is on a budget pension before applying this skill's fund-based figures.
