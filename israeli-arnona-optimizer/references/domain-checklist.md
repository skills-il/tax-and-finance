# Domain Checklist: israeli-arnona-optimizer

Scope: estimate municipal arnona, check discount eligibility, draft objection (hasaga) letters. Category: tax-and-finance.

Reviewed 2026-08-19 against the consolidated text of the Arrangements in the State Economy Regulations (Arnona Discount), 5753-1993, and the Senior Citizens Law 5750-1989. Re-reviewed 2026-10-07 against Reshumot (Kovetz HaTakanot 12366, 12490, 12499), the fiscal-2026 tzav arnona of eleven cities, the 1976 Objection Law, the 1980 Interest Law, the Municipalities Ordinance and a Kol Zchut aggregator sweep.

## Must cover (core)

- Per-municipality rate structure read from each city's own tzav: zone AND building type or size class (never a single per-zone rate), size bands that reprice the whole flat (Haifa), marginal tiers (Ashdod), and the annual national update coefficient (1.626% for 2026, 3.05% for 2027).
- Who the holder (machzik) is, the written notice that ends liability, and seller / landlord liability on a sale or lease (Municipalities Ordinance s.1, s.325, s.326).
- Senior Citizens Law s.13A low-pension seniors (100%, as for income support) and the s.9(e) reporting duty.
- Household composition for the income test: everyone living in the flat, their combined gross income, and the household-size row; a divorced parent's row depends on where the children live.
- The **ceiling vs entitlement** distinction. Regulation 2 and Regulations 3f, 3g, 7 and 14c set CEILINGS a council may choose within; Chapter Hey2 (14e, 14e1), Reg. 3c and Reg. 3c1, and Senior Citizens Law s.9 are ENTITLEMENTS. Presenting a ceiling as an entitlement is a defect, not a simplification.
- **Every row of the discount table, each as its own item.** A one-line "discount eligibility" entry is what let ten statutory rows go missing for four versions:
  - 2(1)(a) senior on pension, up to 25%, 100 sqm
  - 2(1)(b) the same senior with an income-support benefit, up to 100%, 100 sqm
  - 2(2) earning incapacity 75%+ with a full benefit, up to 80%
  - 2(3) proven medical disability 90%+, up to 40%
  - 2(4) the six-item persecution and Prisoner-of-Zion pension list (Prisoner of Zion / Hanged of the Kingdom, Nazi Persecution Invalids Law, German BEG, Dutch WUV, Austrian OFG, Belgian 1954), up to 66%, 70 sqm rising to 90
  - 2(5) blind person's certificate, up to 90%
  - 2(6)(a) and (b) oleh and oleh-citizen, up to 90%, 100 sqm, 12 of 24 months
  - 2(6a) oleh dependent on the help of others, up to 80%
  - 2(6b) SLA member and spouse, up to 90%, 100 sqm, 12 of 36 months
  - 2(7)(c) nursing benefit, up to 70%
  - 2(8) income test with the FULL First Schedule table by household size, all four columns, for BOTH the current fiscal year and the next one once published (fiscal 2027 published 28.7.2026, in force 1.1.2027), and which calendar year's income each tests
  - 2(9) Righteous Among the Nations and spouse, up to 66%
  - 2(10) single parent, up to 20%
  - 2(11) parent of a child (including a foster child) entitled to the disabled-child benefit, up to 33%, 100 sqm, computed PER CHILD up to 90% combined (Arrangements Law s.12(g))
  - Rows outside Reg. 2: Prisoner of Zion on an income-based benefit after 6 months of income support (100%, mandatory, 100 sqm); old-age pension for the disabled within the average-wage test (100%, 100 sqm); income-support and BL maintenance recipients who started before 2003 with no 6-month break (up to 70%)
  - 2(12) released captive, up to 20%
  - 3c Gaza envelope, 45% residential and 39% other, FY2015 to FY2026, plus the 7 km rule
  - 3c1 evacuated locality, 100% from 07.10.2023
  - 3d shmita agricultural land, 90 to 100%
  - 3f active reserve soldier, up to 5%
  - 3g active reserve COMMANDER, up to 25%, 100 sqm
  - 7 needy holder (nazak), up to 70%
  - 12 new empty building, up to 100% for up to 12 months
  - 13 empty building ladder, 100 / 66.66 / 50 across 6 / 12 / 36 cumulative months from 01.03.2005
  - 14 new industry, 10% to 75% by holding year and local unemployment
  - 14c senior business owner, home rate on the first 40 sqm of the business, 240,000 NIS turnover cap
  - 14e(1) conscript, national service, civilian-security service, 100%; (1a) three quarters; (2) two thirds for disabled veterans, Nazi-war invalids, police, prison service, bereaved families, hostile-action casualties; (3) 50% split tracks
  - 14e1 hostage or missing person, 100%
  - Senior Citizens Law s.9, 30% and 100%, with the average-wage and 150%-of-average-wage income tests
- **Area caps are per row.** 100 sqm, 70 sqm rising to 90 where more than four family members live with the holder (Reg. 14f and Reg. 2(4)), or no cap at all. A blanket 100 sqm is wrong.
- The stacking and payment rules: Reg. 17(a) one discount only and none to a second holder, 17(a1) the single cumulative exception, 17(b) one property only, 17(c) pro rata by months held, Reg. 20 payment precondition, Reg. 16 cancellation on an unpaid 31 December balance, and the Reg. 3c1(c) and 14h carve-outs from those.
- Objection (hasaga) process under the 1976 Objection Law: the four closed grounds of s.3(a), 90 days from receipt of the payment notice, the manager's 60 days with DEEMED ACCEPTANCE on silence (s.4), 30 days to the appeals committee, then the Administrative Court; filing does not suspend payment; a refused discount is not an objection ground.
- Arrears: the 1980 Interest Law (30-day trigger, linked arrears interest, oldest-debt-first, the one-year arnona instalment limit of s.4(a) with CPI linkage; the two-year plans of s.4(b) are for Schedule levies only), refunds of overpayment (s.6(a)); the 7-year limitation on uncollected debt; the s.330 destroyed-building exemption.
- A calculator whose every constant traces to a numbered paragraph or to a named city's published tzav.
- Gotchas: per-municipality rate variance, discount renewal and expiry, residential-vs-commercial classification, the appeal deadline, ceiling-vs-entitlement, and the absence of national student and large-family rows.

## Should cover (advanced)

- Payment options (bimonthly, annual lump-sum discount, direct debit, arrears plans).
- Appeal letter drafting with statutory references; surveyor re-measurement.
- Reg. 2a: the council may set sub-tests on the material position of the holder for paragraphs 2(1) to (10).
- Reg. 22a: since amendment 5786-2 a council may not resolve on discounts where the revenue loss would create a current budget deficit.
- Reg. 4: which paragraphs require a Form 1 application and which do not.
- Deferred 2026-10-07, not yet covered: the urban-renewal replacement-flat discount (Urban Renewal Authority Law s.17(b), own income table and 5-year ladder); collection enforcement under the Taxes (Collection) Ordinance; Arrangements Law s.12(f) disabled person housed in another person's holding; the 2007 Kfar Azar and Kiryat Haim sub-tables.

## Out of scope (explicit)

- Income tax, VAT, national insurance. Other authorities entirely. Re-litigated 2026-10-07 (unchanged): an arnona user does not plausibly ask this skill to compute them, and each has its own skill. Stays out.
- A live per-municipality discount table showing where each council actually sits within each ceiling. Re-litigated 2026-10-07: a user WOULD plausibly ask, and for the eleven cities whose tzav is now read it IS capturable (each tzav has a discounts chapter). Reopened as deferred work rather than closed: this cycle captured the rate tables, and the discount chapters of the same eleven documents are the next cycle's job. For the other ~240 authorities the structural answer stands: name the ceiling, say the council chooses within it, send the user to their own tzav.
- Student and large-family rates (re-litigated 2026-10-07, unchanged). Not out of scope by choice: the national regulation contains no such paragraph. The skill says so explicitly rather than staying silent, because silence is what agents fill with invented figures.
