# Domain Checklist: israeli-arnona-optimizer

Scope: estimate municipal arnona, check discount eligibility, draft objection (hasaga) letters. Category: tax-and-finance.

Reviewed 2026-08-19 against the consolidated text of the Arrangements in the State Economy Regulations (Arnona Discount), 5753-1993, and the Senior Citizens Law 5750-1989.

## Must cover (core)

- Per-municipality rate structure (zones, usage types, sqm) plus the annual national update coefficient.
- The **ceiling vs entitlement** distinction. Regulation 2 and Regulations 3f, 3g, 7 and 14c set CEILINGS a council may choose within; Chapter Hey2 (14e, 14e1), Reg. 3c and Reg. 3c1, and Senior Citizens Law s.9 are ENTITLEMENTS. Presenting a ceiling as an entitlement is a defect, not a simplification.
- **Every row of the discount table, each as its own item.** A one-line "discount eligibility" entry is what let ten statutory rows go missing for four versions:
  - 2(1)(a) senior on pension, up to 25%, 100 sqm
  - 2(1)(b) the same senior with an income-support benefit, up to 100%, 100 sqm
  - 2(2) earning incapacity 75%+ with a full benefit, up to 80%
  - 2(3) proven medical disability 90%+, up to 40%
  - 2(4) the six-item persecution and Prisoner-of-Zion pension list (Prisoner of Zion / Hanged of the Kingdom, Nazi Persecution Invalids Law, German BEG, Dutch WUV, Austrian OFG, Belgian 1954), up to 66%, 70 sqm rising to 90
  - 2(5) blind person's certificate, up to 90%
  - 2(6)(a) and (b) oleh and oleh-citizen, up to 90%, 100 sqm, 12 of 24 months
  - 2(6a) oleh dependent on the help of others, up to 80%
  - 2(6b) SLA member and spouse, up to 90%, 100 sqm, 12 of 36 months
  - 2(7)(c) nursing benefit, up to 70%
  - 2(8) income test with the FULL First Schedule table by household size, all four columns
  - 2(9) Righteous Among the Nations and spouse, up to 66%
  - 2(10) single parent, up to 20%
  - 2(11) parent of a child (including a foster child) entitled to the disabled-child benefit, up to 33%, 100 sqm
  - 2(12) released captive, up to 20%
  - 3c Gaza envelope, 45% residential and 39% other, FY2015 to FY2026, plus the 7 km rule
  - 3c1 evacuated locality, 100% from 07.10.2023
  - 3d shmita agricultural land, 90 to 100%
  - 3f active reserve soldier, up to 5%
  - 3g active reserve COMMANDER, up to 25%, 100 sqm
  - 7 needy holder (nazak), up to 70%
  - 12 new empty building, up to 100% for up to 12 months
  - 13 empty building ladder, 100 / 66.66 / 50 across 6 / 12 / 36 cumulative months from 01.03.2005
  - 14 new industry, 10% to 75% by holding year and local unemployment
  - 14c senior business owner, home rate on the first 40 sqm of the business, 240,000 NIS turnover cap
  - 14e(1) conscript, national service, civilian-security service, 100%; (1a) three quarters; (2) two thirds for disabled veterans, Nazi-war invalids, police, prison service, bereaved families, hostile-action casualties; (3) 50% split tracks
  - 14e1 hostage or missing person, 100%
  - Senior Citizens Law s.9, 30% and 100%, with the average-wage and 150%-of-average-wage income tests
- **Area caps are per row.** 100 sqm, 70 sqm rising to 90 where more than four family members live with the holder (Reg. 14f and Reg. 2(4)), or no cap at all. A blanket 100 sqm is wrong.
- The stacking and payment rules: Reg. 17(a) one discount only and none to a second holder, 17(a1) the single cumulative exception, 17(b) one property only, 17(c) pro rata by months held, Reg. 20 payment precondition, Reg. 16 cancellation on an unpaid 31 December balance, and the Reg. 3c1(c) and 14h carve-outs from those.
- Objection (hasaga) process with the correct deadlines: 90 days to file with the manager, 60 days for the manager to respond, 30 days to appeal to the committee, then the Administrative Court (1976 objection law).
- A calculator whose every constant traces to a numbered paragraph.
- Gotchas: per-municipality rate variance, discount renewal and expiry, residential-vs-commercial classification, the appeal deadline, ceiling-vs-entitlement, and the absence of national student and large-family rows.

## Should cover (advanced)

- Payment options (bimonthly, annual lump-sum discount, direct debit, arrears plans).
- Appeal letter drafting with statutory references; surveyor re-measurement.
- Reg. 2a: the council may set sub-tests on the material position of the holder for paragraphs 2(1) to (10).
- Reg. 22a: since amendment 5786-2 a council may not resolve on discounts where the revenue loss would create a current budget deficit.
- Reg. 4: which paragraphs require a Form 1 application and which do not.

## Out of scope (explicit)

- Income tax, VAT, national insurance. Other authorities entirely. Re-litigated 2026-08-19: an arnona user does not plausibly ask this skill to compute them, and each has its own skill. Stays out.
- A live per-municipality discount table showing where each council actually sits within each ceiling. Re-litigated 2026-08-19: a user WOULD plausibly ask, and the honest answer is that it is not published in one place and would need a scrape of ~250 tzav-arnona documents per year. The skill now answers it structurally instead of by silence: it names the ceiling, states that the council chooses within it, and sends the user to their own council's table. Revisit if a consolidated Ministry of Interior dataset appears.
- Student and large-family rates. Not out of scope by choice: the national regulation contains no such paragraph. The skill says so explicitly rather than staying silent, because silence is what agents fill with invented figures.
