# Screening criteria

Checklists only. Every figure is backed by `evidence.json`. Nothing here is tax advice, and
none of it classifies any product.

## A. PFIC screen

A foreign corporation is a PFIC if it meets EITHER test.

| Test | Threshold | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Income | 75 percent or more of gross income for the tax year is passive income | section 1297(b) |
| Asset | At least 50 percent of average assets produce, or are held to produce, passive income | section 1297(e) |

Filing exception to check FIRST:

- [ ] PFIC stock owned is worth USD 25,000 or less (USD 50,000 or less on a joint return)
- [ ] measured on the last day of the tax year, AND
- [ ] on any day during the year on which stock was disposed of

If all three hold, the Form 8621 filing exception applies. It is an exception to FILING only.
It does not change how a distribution or gain is taxed and does not affect any other duty.

Default regime if no election is in place: the holding is a section 1291 fund, an excess
distribution is subject to special rules, and the ENTIRE gain on disposition is treated as an
excess distribution. Elections that avoid it (QEF, mark-to-market) are a preparer's call, and
a QEF election needs fund-supplied information that Israeli funds generally do not produce.

## B. Foreign trust screen, Revenue Procedure 2020-17

**Scope first.** The exemption is from the section 6048 information reporting requirements
only. It does not change taxation. A product can be exempt from Forms 3520 and 3520-A and
still produce currently taxable income.

**Gate.** Only eligible individuals, generally those already compliant with the income tax
obligations relating to the trust, may rely on it.

### Section 5.03, tax-favored foreign retirement trust

- [ ] Operates exclusively or almost exclusively to provide, or earn income for, pension or
      retirement benefits and ancillary or incidental benefits
- [ ] (1) Generally exempt from income tax or otherwise tax-favored locally
- [ ] (2) Annual information reporting is provided or available to the local tax authority
- [ ] (3) Only contributions with respect to income earned from personal services are permitted
- [ ] (4) Contributions limited by a percentage of earned income, OR to USD 50,000 or less
      annually, OR to USD 1,000,000 or less lifetime
- [ ] (5) Withdrawals conditioned on reaching a specified retirement age, disability, or death,
      or penalties apply to earlier withdrawals. Carve-out only for in-service loans, hardship,
      educational purposes, or purchase of a primary residence
- [ ] (6) For an employer-maintained trust, nondiscriminatory across a wide range of employees

Criterion (5) is the one most Israeli non-pension products fail.

### Section 5.04, tax-favored foreign non-retirement savings trust

- [ ] Operates exclusively or almost exclusively to provide MEDICAL, DISABILITY, or
      EDUCATIONAL benefits
- [ ] (1) Locally tax-favored as defined in 5.03(1)
- [ ] (2) Annual information reporting available to the local tax authority
- [ ] (3) Contributions limited to USD 10,000 or less annually, or USD 200,000 or less lifetime
- [ ] (4) Withdrawals conditioned on provision of medical, disability, or educational benefits,
      or penalties apply

5.04 has its OWN purpose test. Failing 5.03 does not move a general savings product into 5.04.

## C. Forms

| Form | Who files | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| 8621 | The US shareholder | Ownership of PFIC stock, subject to the de minimis exception |
| 3520 | The US person | Certain transactions with foreign trusts, ownership under sections 671 to 679, and certain large foreign gifts or bequests |
| 3520-A | The foreign trust with at least one US owner | Annual information about the trust, its US beneficiaries and US owners |

## D. Conversion

Every threshold above is in US dollars and every Israeli contribution is in shekels. Convert
per tax year and record the rate and its date. A product can sit on either side of a limit
depending on the year's rate.
