# Diligence Question Bank

Pick the 5 to 10 questions that resolve the biggest open risks for THIS deal. Do
not send all of them. A focused list signals a sharp investor; a 40-question
generic form signals a tourist.

## Team and founders
- How long have you worked together, and how did you split equity and why?
- Is everyone full-time? What is the vesting / reverse-vesting on founder shares?
- What is the one thing about this market only you know?
- Who has left the cap table or the team, and why?

## Market and timing
- What is your bottom-up TAM (number of buyers times realistic ACV), not top-down?
- Why is now the moment, what specifically changed?
- Who is the buyer, what is the budget line, and what do they use today?

## Product and moat
- What is built and in production vs roadmap? Can I see it live?
- What stops a well-funded competitor (or the incumbent) from copying this in 6 months?
- Where does the core IP legally sit, and is any of it encumbered (grants, prior employer, university)?

## Traction and metrics (request the raw data room)
- Define your ARR exactly: contracted, recurring, annualized from which months?
- Monthly revenue and logo count for the last 12 to 18 months (a chart, with absolute numbers).
- Gross margin, net revenue retention, logo churn, CAC and CAC payback.
- Current monthly net burn, cash in bank, and runway before and after this round.
- Which "customers" are paying vs pilots / design partners / LOIs?

## Deal, cap table, structure
- Current cap table (fully diluted), including all outstanding SAFEs/notes and their caps.
- Option pool size now and the post-round target, and is the top-up pre or post-money?
- What pre/post-money are you raising on, and who (if anyone) is leading / has committed?
- Any secondary in this round? Any unusual terms from prior rounds?

## Terms and control
- What liquidation preference applies (1x non-participating, participating, a multiple),
  and what is the full preference stack from prior rounds?
- What anti-dilution protection do existing investors have (weighted-average, full ratchet)?
- What does the board look like after this round, and what protective / veto provisions
  do investors hold?
- Are there drag-along, tag-along / co-sale, ROFR, or pro-rata rights, and on what terms?

## Israel-specific (ask when the structure or grants suggest exposure)
- Have you taken Israel Innovation Authority grants? How much, which programs, and what
  royalty balance is outstanding?
- Is any know-how or IP subject to IIA approval before it can move abroad, and have you
  modeled the redemption fee on an exit or a flip?
- Are you a single Israeli Ltd, or have you done (or do you plan) a Delaware flip? Where
  is the IP owned today?
- Is your Section 102 option plan under a trustee, capital-gains track, and fully compliant?
- Is the company in good standing with the Companies Registrar (not a "violating company"),
  and are there any registered charges or liens over the company or its IP?
- Has every founder, employee, and contractor signed an IP-assignment agreement, and are
  there any open employee service-invention claims?
- What accrued labor liabilities exist (severance, pension, study fund, unused vacation),
  and are any workers engaged as contractors who function as employees?
- Do you claim Preferred / Preferred Technological Enterprise tax status, and do you meet
  the conditions?

## Closing
- What would you do with this round, and what milestone does it buy you to?
- What is the biggest risk you would flag if you were me?
