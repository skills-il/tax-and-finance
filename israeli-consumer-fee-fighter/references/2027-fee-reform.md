# The 2027 fee reform (circular 06-2851)

Companion to section E of the skill. **None of this is in force yet.** Answer present-day questions from section A.

Source: Bank of Israel, Supervisor of Banks circular 06-2851 of 21 June 2026, "רפורמה בתחום העמלות הנגבות ממשקי בית ועסקים קטנים על שירותי ניהול חשבון תשלום וכרטיס חיוב מיידי (דביט)", <https://www.boi.org.il/media/0cgabv01/h2851.pdf>. The accompanying supervision order is signed 11 June 2026; do not conflate the two dates.

## Dates

| What | When |
|---|---|
| Updated fee rules take effect | 1 July 2027 |
| Debit-card fee supervision order | 1 October 2026 |
| Abolition of the "technical cheque return", "account location" and "estates and inheritance handling" services | 1 October 2026 |
| Early adoption by an individual bank | permitted at any time before the deadline |

Adoption is per-bank until the deadline, so the regime that applies to a given customer is a question about their bank, not about the calendar.

## What changes

- **One service replaces three tracks.** The basic, expanded and expanded-plus tracks are abolished and replaced by a single supervised service, "ניהול חשבון תשלום" (payment-account management), bundling the common day-to-day operations.
- **Price caps**, per the order:
  - 0 to 2 operations in the month: up to 5 NIS
  - 3 to 100 operations in the month: up to 10 NIS
  - each additional operation beyond the first 100: up to 1 NIS
- **Enrolment becomes automatic and opt-out.** Every customer is joined to the service without having to act. This removes the central trap in section A, where a customer who never chose a track silently paid per action.
- **The teller-versus-direct-channel distinction disappears**, because the new service covers both.
- **Debit-card fee capped at 7 NIS**, against a market average of about 9 NIS. It replaces the 2015 arrangement waiving the fee for 36 months for customers who also held a credit card, and it applies to cards issued before the change.
- **Directive 423 ("the tracks service") is cancelled.**
- **Mid-month closure still costs a full month.** Where the account closes mid-month, whoever initiated it, the bank may charge the whole monthly cost for that month.

## What this does to the section A entitlements

The rules delete the defined terms "אזרח ותיק" and "אדם עם מוגבלות" (both marked `תבוטל ביום 1.7.2027` in the consolidated text) and repeal the track-related limbs of rule 4a, including the 4a(b1) and 4a(b2) automatic-enrolment true-up.

Note the mechanism precisely, because it is easy to state wrongly: rule 4a is amended rather than repealed in its entirety, and the 4-teller-actions-at-direct-price entitlement never lived in rule 4a at all. It is the note to First Schedule item 1(a)(2). It falls because it is written in terms of the two deleted definitions, not because rule 4a goes.

Do not assume either entitlement carries over into a bank's post-reform tariff in some other form. Check the tariff.
