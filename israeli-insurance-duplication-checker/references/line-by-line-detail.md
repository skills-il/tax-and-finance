# Line-by-line detail

Long-form material behind Steps D, D2 and E of SKILL.md. SKILL.md carries the decision; this file carries the wording, the statutory hooks and the caveats.

## The שאירים waiver (behind Step D)

**The one pension line that CAN be pure waste: שאירים cover with no שאירים.** The skill's default is that fixed-sum cover stacks and should be left alone, and that default is right almost everywhere. It has one real exception, and an agent that never raises it leaves money on the table every month. A member with no survivors other than a parent may waive the death-risk cover: "עמית מבוטח ללא שאירים למעט שאיר שהוא הורה... רשאי לוותר על כיסוי ביטוחי לסיכוני מוות עבור שאיריו למשך תקופה שלא תעלה על 24 חודשים", renewable "כל עוד אין לו שאירים". A separate route lets a member with children but no spouse waive only the spouse component. During the waiver "לא ייגבו מהעמית דמי ביטוח בשל כיסוי ביטוחי למקרה מוות", and since insurance premiums are one of the items deducted from the member's יתרה צבורה, not charging them stops that deduction. Note the definition that decides eligibility: a יתום is a child "שטרם מלאו לו 21 שנים", or a child with a disability.

**And the trap inside that exception, which is the reason to raise it carefully rather than enthusiastically.** The waiver looks reversible, and on paper it is: at the end of the period the member returns to "מסלול הביטוח או בשיעור הכיסוי הביטוחי, שהיה לו בטרם הסתיימה תקופת הוויתור". But the fund is expressly permitted to run חיתום רפואי on "הגדלת הכיסוי הביטוחי כך שיכלול גם כיסוי ביטוחי לשאירים, לגבי עמית שוויתר", and on the strength of it may "לדחות בקשה... וכן רשאית להחריג מהכיסוי הביטוחי... כל מצב רפואי". The sole exemption is a member who kept paying for the default ביטוח המשך continuation cover throughout, which cancels the אכשרה, and which the member is also allowed to opt out of.

So this is not a free saving. It is exactly the safe-cut rule again, one level down: waiving without keeping the continuation cover is a one-way door for anyone whose health changes during the waiver. Raise the waiver, state the underwriting risk in the same breath, and tell the user to confirm the continuation-cover position with the fund before signing anything. Do not present the waiver as an obvious win.


## סיעוד, the three things users conflate (behind Step D2)

There are three separate things, and users conflate all three:

1. **גמלת סיעוד from ביטוח לאומי.** A statutory benefit under חוק הביטוח הלאומי, פרק י', not an insurance policy and not something anyone bought. It is income-tested at the HOUSEHOLD level: for a couple, a reduced benefit starts above roughly 20,000 a month, and "כאשר משק הבית מרוויח מעל כ-30,000 ש\"ח בחודש, שני בני הזוג במשק הבית אינם זכאים כלל לגמלת סיעוד".
2. **The kupa group policy.** Arranged by the kupat cholim as בעלת הפוליסה for its members, under תקנות הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (ביטוח) (ביטוח סיעודי קבוצתי לחברי קופת חולים), התשע"ו-2015 and חוזר ביטוח 2016-1-3. Terms are identical across kupot; the premium varies by kupa and by the insured's age. This is the two-tier benefit described above: פיצוי at home, שיפוי in an institution. These policies are bought "נוסף על גמלת הסיעוד של בט\"ל", not instead of it.
3. **A legacy private policy.** About a million Israelis hold individual סיעוד cover "אשר נרכשו בעבר ואינם נמכרים יותר בשוק".

That last sentence is the whole verdict. **An individual סיעוד policy cannot be repurchased at any price, because the product is no longer sold at all.** The safe-cut rule is at its absolute sharpest here: this is not merely underwritten cover that a sick user might fail to re-buy, it is cover that a perfectly healthy user could not re-buy either. Never put a legacy private סיעוד policy at the top of a cancellation list. If something has to go and the group and private policies genuinely overlap, the reversible layer is the group one, because kupa membership is not underwritten.

Scale, for context when a user asks whether this is really worth checking: "שוק הביטוח הסיעודי בישראל כולל כ-5 מיליון מבוטחים בביטוח סיעודי-קבוצתי דרך קופות החולים", about half the population.

Two things NOT to say, because they are widely repeated and this skill does not have them sourced:

- Do NOT tell the user the kupot group policy is being wound up on a stated date. A Ministry of Health draft letter circulated in January 2025 proposed halting new sales and then operation, never issued in final form, was not coordinated with the insurance regulator, and was dropped. Treat the arrangement as operating and tell the user to confirm current status with their kupa.
- Do NOT assert that receiving גמלת סיעוד from ביטוח לאומי does or does not reduce a policy payout. The sources establish the two run in parallel; they do not settle the payout interaction. This is a question for the policy wording and the insurer.


## Mortgage cover and the pupils' policy (behind Step E)

- **Mortgage cover is the most under-audited property line in the country, and Directive 451 hands the user three separate levers.** A bank may require the borrower to insure the property and to take life cover up to the loan amount, "ובלבד שהתאגיד הבנקאי יהא מוטב בלתי חוזר". But: (a) the bank must tell the borrower he may buy both directly rather than through the bank's own agency, and that notice must appear "באופן בולט ובאותיות גדולות" in the explanatory pages, in the loan agreement and in the annual notice, so a borrower paying the bank agency's premium is very often paying more than they need to; (b) the customer "יכול לבטל את הפוליסה בכל עת ללא עמלה, אם יציג בפני התאגיד הבנקאי פוליסת ביטוח חילופית שהתאגיד הבנקאי הוא המוטב הבלתי חוזר בה", which is the mechanism for switching without losing the loan; (c) the bank "לא יחייב לווה בביטוח הנכס המשועבד בערך העולה על יתרת ההלוואה המשוערכת הבלתי מסולקת", and must offer that lower-value option in a separate document. There is also no obligation to insure loans "עד 30,000 ש\"ח או שיתרתן אינה עולה על סכום זה, לרבות האפשרות לבטל ביטוחים קיימים אלה".
- **ביטוח מבנה taken with the mortgage against a separate ביטוח דירה is a ס' 59 question, not a health-style one.** Both insure one property against one risk, so if the sums together exceed the value of the property unreasonably, either the insured or an insurer may "לדרוש הפחתת סכומי הביטוח עד לשווי הנכס", with premiums falling correspondingly from the date of demand. Put it in REAL WASTE only after confirming both policies actually cover the structure rather than one covering only תכולה. When cancelling or reducing, the bank's charge has to be released: the borrower applies to the insurer to reduce the sum or cancel the charge, and the bank "ימסור ללווה אישור ייעודי, ממוען לחברת הביטוח" consenting. Never send a cancellation on a mortgage-linked policy before that confirmation exists, or the bank may treat the loan as in breach.
- **ביטוח תאונות אישיות לתלמידים is already paid for, and parents routinely buy it twice.** Every child entitled to free education "יהיה מבוטח בביטוח תאונות אישיות, באמצעות רשות החינוך המקומית", under a tendered contract, with the premium fixed nationally rather than by the school. It is not school-hours cover: the policy pays for injury "שאירע במקום כלשהו בשטח מדינת ישראל והשטחים המוחזקים, בכל שעה משעות היממה בין שיש לה קשר לפעילות המוסד החינוכי ובין אם לא". For 2025/26 the premium is 69 ₪ with no deductible. A private child personal-accident rider bought on top is fixed-sum, so it does legitimately stack and is not unlawful, but the parent should be told plainly what the 69 ₪ already buys before deciding the rider is necessary.

## Critical illness and dental overlap (behind Step C)

- מחלות קשות: fixed sum, so it cannot duplicate anything, and no one should cancel it because an agent called it overlap. But "leave it alone" is not an audit. Households commonly hold this cover in four places at once: a standalone policy, a rider on a private health policy, an employer group benefit, and a rider inside a ביטוח מנהלים. Four premiums, and four covered-condition lists that differ materially in which conditions qualify and what survival period applies. The right output is not "stacks, keep everything" but "stacks, so compare the condition lists and the sums, and cut on price only where two lists are near-identical". That is a PARTIAL verdict, and it is the one place the skill applies PARTIAL to fixed-sum cover.
- שיניים: check it, because a large part of it may already be free. The public basket covers "טיפולי שיניים לילדים שטרם מלאו להם 18 שנים", and separately "טיפולי שיניים משמרים ומונעים למבוטחים שמלאו להם 72 שנים", with the periodic check-up, bitewing pair and scaling carried "בלא השתתפות עצמית". A dental policy bought for a nine-year-old, or for a grandparent over 72, is buying a lot of what the basket already provides. Dental benefits are normally paid as שיפוי, so the שב"ן-plus-policy overlap is a CAPPED verdict, not stacking. The real non-basket exception is אורתודנטיה, which the basket's own children's trauma item expressly carves out ("ולמעט אורתודנטיה"), so orthodontic cover belongs in PARTIAL rather than REAL WASTE.


## אכ"ע mechanics: the two 75% rules, the 100% offset and the 30% floor (behind Step D)

The two 75% rules are different instruments on different bases, and the skill must say which one it means:

- Inside a pension or provident fund: "סכום קצבת הנכות לא יעלה על 75% מן השכר המבוטח בקופת הגמל".
- In the uniform private policy: 75% of the average salary insured under that plan.

Cross-insurer offset does NOT trigger at 75%. It triggers only when total monthly benefit from all insurers exceeds "100% ממוצע השכר של המבוטח מכל מקור הכנסה", and even then the private policy still pays a floor: "הפיצוי החודשי שישולם למבוטח לא יפחת, בכל מקרה, משיעור של 30% מסכום הפיצוי החודשי לו הוא זכאי על פי תנאי התכנית". So the band above the per-policy cap and up to 100% of income is legitimate cover, not waste. A pension fund counts as a "מבטח אחר" for this purpose.

Excess-premium refund exists in the uniform policy but is capped at seven years AND conditional on a claim: "לא קרה מקרה הביטוח, המבוטח לא יהיה זכאי להחזר פרמיה". Never claim, never get it back. Tell the user this rather than letting them assume the money is banked.

Two related rules worth surfacing: at the point of sale the insurer must check the מסלקה, must refuse to sell against income that is already insured, and must cancel a duplicate (חוזר ביטוח 2018-1-8). And an אכ"ע premium may never be funded out of מרכיב הפיצויים.


## Travel cover and card benefits (behind Step F)

- Charging the trip to the card is generally NOT a condition on Israeli cards, unlike the US and UK model, and the belief that it is may be the most repeated false belief in Israeli consumer content here. Treat this as the default, not a universal: confirm it on the specific card, because one product conditioning on it would leave a user who relied on the rule with no cover. What matters is that the card is valid, and for discount benefits, that the policy premium is paid on that card.
- Most Israeli cards give a DISCOUNT, not embedded cover, typically a few free days and then a percentage off. Some premium tiers do embed cover and some issuers have discontinued it. This skill holds no sourced per-card list, so do not name products: send the user to their own card's benefit page.
- Everything that is not embedded requires activation before every single trip. The classic failure is believing the card covers you and never activating.
- The six-month pre-existing-condition exclusion is statutory, not underwriter discretion. It excludes "מחלה שבשלה היה המבוטח בטיפול או בהשגחה בעת צאתו לחוץ לארץ או במשך ששת החדשים שקדמו לצאתו".
- A standalone policy is genuinely needed on top of a card benefit for: pre-existing conditions, pregnancy, extreme and winter sports, trips longer than the age-banded day caps, ביטול נסיעה for non-medical reasons (absent from every card product examined), high-value gear above the כבודה sub-limits, and ages 80 and over.
- Do not invent per-card specifics. Give the user a checklist of what to verify on their own card's benefit page.
- Travel policies do not appear in הר הביטוח.


## Statutory mechanics behind the three-tier table

The mechanics behind the table, which the agent should be able to state when challenged:

- ס' 59 is the ביטוח כפל provision, not ס' 56. Its own text is asset-bound: "בוטח נכס בפני סיכון אחד אצל יותר ממבטח אחד לתקופות חופפות". ס' 67 extends it to ביטוח אחריות, so liability cover is caught too, and ס' 64 makes it non-derogable except in the insured's favour.
- Under ס' 59(ג) the insurers are liable "יחד ולחוד לגבי סכום הביטוח החופף". The insured is not left chasing two companies; they simply cannot collect the overlap twice, and the insurers settle the split between themselves.
- ס' 54(ב) imports only ss. 42, 49, 52, 56, 61, 62 and 64 into loss-based accident, illness and disability cover. ס' 59 is absent from that list. What actually caps reimbursement health cover is ס' 56(א), "חובת השיפוי של המבטח תהיה כשיעור הנזק שנגרם".
- ס' 54(א) routes fixed-sum cover to פרק ב', which contains no כפל provision and no indemnity ceiling. So a standalone ריסק, a mortgage life policy and the שאירים cover inside the pension all pay on the same death. Say so plainly. A user who cancels one of them because an agent called it "duplication" has lost cover for nothing.


## ותק, אכשרה and cancellation mechanics (behind Steps G and H)

### Step G. The ותק and אכשרה trap, before anyone cancels anything

- Switching kupa preserves שב"ן ותק only on joining the new kupa's שב"ן within 90 days, and only between parallel tiers; moving up a tier means serving the receiving kupa's אכשרה for the upgrade.
- Cancelling and re-joining the SAME kupa restarts the waiting periods from scratch.
- Current tier names, because users and marketing material both garble them: מכבי has כסף, זהב and שלי (three cumulative tiers); כללית has מושלם זהב and מושלם פלטינום; מאוחדת has עדיף and שיא (there is no "זהב" at מאוחדת); לאומית has כסף and זהב. These are marketing names and they change, so confirm the current tier list on the kupa's own site before writing it into a report rather than treating this list as authoritative.

**Check residency before applying the safe-cut rule at all.** The rule assumes the kupa must re-accept the user. That fails for anyone whose entitlement under חוק ביטוח בריאות ממלכתי is unsettled, and for them the private policy may be the ONLY cover rather than the redundant layer. Stop and ask about status for a new oleh, a תושב חוזר, someone on relocation still holding Israeli policies, a dual citizen with foreign cover, or a non-resident spouse. A foreign employer's global plan alongside שב"ן is a real overlap worth mapping, but it is also the layer that disappears when the posting ends. This skill does not carry the waiting-period rules for any of these statuses: establish status, then have the user confirm entitlement before anything is cancelled.

### Step H. Cancellation mechanics

- The 14-day distance-selling withdrawal right does NOT apply to insurance. חוק הגנת הצרכן excludes insurers entirely. This is widely believed and false, and a user who relies on it will be caught out.
- The real instrument is חוזר ביטוח 2017-1-3, in force since 1.7.2017: every insurer must run a front-page area titled "ביטול פוליסה קיימת", plus email, personal-account, phone and fax routes, an automatic acknowledgement, and three business days to report deficiencies.
- Cancellation by the INSURED takes effect three days after the notice reaches the insurer: "מתבטל החוזה כעבור שלושה ימים מהיום שבו נמסרה הודעת הביטול למבטח". The fifteen days often quoted is the insurer's side. Do not swap them.
- Life insurance may be cancelled at any time by written notice.
- שב"ן cancellation notice is set by each kupa's תקנון, not by statute. Check the specific kupa.
- An insurer may not sell a duplicate פיצוי policy unless the insured confirms in writing after being told they will be charged for both (חוזר צירוף 2016-1-7). And a הר הביטוח lookup is required before every sale, with no opt-out at all in שיווק יזום.

## The שקל ראשון reform (behind Step C)

Handle the reform correctly, because most secondary sources do not. It took effect in **June 2024**; February 2016 is only the purchase-date cutoff defining which policies were caught, so never call it a 2016 event. It applied to פוליסות פרט only, and the reversal window ran to 31 May 2025, a year from the transfer, though some insurers extended their own, so check rather than assume it is closed. **The GROUP market was affected far less and with delay**, because group policies update only every few years under the policyholder-insurer agreement, so much group surgery cover is still מהשקל הראשון. Do NOT say group policies were never converted; have the user check their own. Anyone who bought individually before February 2016 was not caught either. Those two populations are this skill's core audience, so ask explicitly whether the policy is individual or group and when it was bought, and never write the health section as though the reform solved the problem. Group cover also has a five-year structural limit and a conversion right on leaving, at a much higher individual premium. Detail in `references/line-by-line-detail.md`.


## Contents riders and service contracts (behind Step E)

- תכולה riders are PARTIAL, not waste. The standard policy caps jewellery and excludes theft outside the home; a כל הסיכונים rider lifts exactly those limits. Ask what it covers before calling it redundant.
- כתבי שירות are the highest-yield manual audit here. Towing, home trades and windscreen typically sit in three or four places at once: a policy rider, a card benefit, a direct subscription, an employer or utility bundle. Service contracts typically carry a clawback and a no-double-benefits term, so the user gets the tow once and the extras deliver only monthly charges. הר הביטוח lists none of them.
