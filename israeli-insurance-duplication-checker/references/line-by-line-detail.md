# Line-by-line detail

Long-form material behind Steps A and C to H of SKILL.md and the deliverables schema. SKILL.md carries the decision; this file carries the wording, the statutory hooks and the caveats.

## The שאירים waiver (behind Step D)

**The one pension line that CAN be pure waste: שאירים cover with no שאירים.** The skill's default is that fixed-sum cover stacks and should be left alone, and that default is right almost everywhere. It has one real exception, and an agent that never raises it leaves money on the table every month. A member with no survivors other than a parent may waive the death-risk cover: "עמית מבוטח ללא שאירים למעט שאיר שהוא הורה... רשאי לוותר על כיסוי ביטוחי לסיכוני מוות עבור שאיריו למשך תקופה שלא תעלה על 24 חודשים", renewable "כל עוד אין לו שאירים". A separate route lets a member with children but no spouse waive only the spouse component. During the waiver "לא ייגבו מהעמית דמי ביטוח בשל כיסוי ביטוחי למקרה מוות", and since insurance premiums are one of the items deducted from the member's יתרה צבורה, not charging them stops that deduction. Note the definition that decides eligibility: a יתום is a child "שטרם מלאו לו 21 שנים", or a child with a disability.

**And the trap inside that exception, which is the reason to raise it carefully rather than enthusiastically.** The waiver looks reversible, and on paper it is: at the end of the period the member returns to "מסלול הביטוח או בשיעור הכיסוי הביטוחי, שהיה לו בטרם הסתיימה תקופת הוויתור". But the fund is expressly permitted to run חיתום רפואי on "הגדלת הכיסוי הביטוחי כך שיכלול גם כיסוי ביטוחי לשאירים, לגבי עמית שוויתר", and on the strength of it may "לדחות בקשה... וכן רשאית להחריג מהכיסוי הביטוחי... כל מצב רפואי". The sole exemption is a member who kept paying for the default ביטוח המשך continuation cover throughout, which cancels the אכשרה, and which the member is also allowed to opt out of.

So this is not a free saving. It is exactly the safe-cut rule again, one level down: waiving without keeping the continuation cover is a one-way door for anyone whose health changes during the waiver. Raise the waiver, state the underwriting risk in the same breath, and tell the user to confirm the continuation-cover position with the fund before signing anything. Do not present the waiver as an obvious win.


## סיעוד, the three things users conflate (behind Step D2)

There are three separate things, and users conflate all three:

1. **גמלת סיעוד from ביטוח לאומי.** A statutory benefit under חוק הביטוח הלאומי, פרק י', not an insurance policy and not something anyone bought. It is income-tested at the HOUSEHOLD level: for a couple, a reduced benefit starts above roughly 20,000 a month, and "כאשר משק הבית מרוויח מעל כ-30,000 ש\"ח בחודש, שני בני הזוג במשק הבית אינם זכאים כלל לגמלת סיעוד".
2. **The kupa group policy.** Arranged by the kupat cholim as בעלת הפוליסה for its members, under תקנות הפיקוח על שירותים פיננסיים (ביטוח) (ביטוח סיעודי קבוצתי לחברי קופת חולים), התשע"ו-2015 and חוזר ביטוח 2016-1-3. Terms are identical across kupot; the premium varies by kupa and by the insured's age. This is the two-tier benefit described above: פיצוי at home, שיפוי in an institution. These policies are bought "נוסף על גמלת הסיעוד של בט\"ל", not instead of it.
3. **A legacy private policy.** About a million Israelis hold individual סיעוד cover "אשר נרכשו בעבר ואינם נמכרים יותר בשוק".

That last sentence is the whole verdict. **An individual סיעוד policy cannot be repurchased at any price, because the product is no longer sold at all.** The safe-cut rule is at its absolute sharpest here: this is not merely underwritten cover that a sick user might fail to re-buy, it is cover that a perfectly healthy user could not re-buy either. Never put a legacy private סיעוד policy at the top of a cancellation list. The kupot group policy is not a free layer either: joining is "בכפוף לבדיקת מצבך הרפואי" and the benefit is fixed by age at FIRST joining (at home 5,000 / 4,100 / 3,200, in an institution 10,000 / 6,500 / 4,500 a month, for a first join up to 49 / 50-59 / 60+). A member who leaves may be refused or come back on a lower tier, so where group and private genuinely overlap neither layer is freely reversible; keep both unless the insurer confirms re-entry terms in writing.

Scale, for context when a user asks whether this is really worth checking: "שוק הביטוח הסיעודי בישראל כולל כ-5 מיליון מבוטחים בביטוח סיעודי-קבוצתי דרך קופות החולים", about half the population.

Two things NOT to say, because they are widely repeated and this skill does not have them sourced:

- Do NOT tell the user the kupot group policy is being wound up on a stated date. A Ministry of Health draft letter circulated in January 2025 proposed halting new sales and then operation, never issued in final form and was not coordinated with the insurance regulator. In September 2025 the Ministry withdrew those dates; its director-general told the Knesset Health Committee, as reported by Calcalist, that he still wanted the kupot out by the end of 2026. Treat the arrangement as operating and tell the user to confirm current status with their kupa.
- Where the kupot group policy stands in 2026, as far as this skill can source it:
  - Eligibility was tightened by the amendment published in December 2024 and in force from 1.1.2025 (events from that date): a claim now needs inability to perform "חלק מהותי של לפחות ארבע מתוך שש פעולות ADL", or three where one is continence ("שלוש פעולות כאשר אחת מהן היא שליטה בסוגרים"), against three of six before. Independent use of aids no longer counts as inability (הראל's own claims guide for events from 1.1.2025: "היעזרות באביזר לצורך ביצוע הפעולה - לא תיחשב כמצב מזכה"). A claimant living at home must also prove they actually receive personal care for most hours of the day. A second route exists on the kupot's own pages: a member whom a specialist declares "תשוש נפש" qualifies without the ADL count. Clalit's page gives the definition: "פגיעה בפעילותו הקוגניטיבית של המבוטח וירידה ביכולתו האינטלקטואלית... הדורשים השגחה במרבית שעות היממה", from conditions such as Alzheimer's or dementias; מכבי's page carries the same route. Confirm it in the user's own policy wording. Clalit also states that the 2025 amendment changed definitions only: "לא יחול שינוי בגובה דמי הביטוח ובסכומי תגמולי הביטוח".
  - **The tightening reaches the kupot group policies only.** The regulator's own notice said "התיקונים יחולו רק לגבי תוכניות ביטוח סיעודי קבוצתי לחברי קופות חולים". A legacy private policy keeps the definition it was written with, commonly the older three-of-six test, so the two instruments now pay in different sets of states. That is one more reason the private policy is not a redundant layer: it pays on a 3-ADL case the group policy refuses.
  - Since December 2023 the insurer carries no insurance risk: claims are paid only from the kupa's members' fund (קרן המבוטחים), which can be depleted, in which case members may get part of the benefit or none.
  - The insurer's duty to move members into a group continuation policy if the kupa does not renew is cancelled from 1.1.2027.
  - Clalit's policy with הראל runs to 31.12.2026. On 16.8.2026 Clalit announced that איילון, the only bidder in its tender, operates the policy from 1.1.2027, for three years with options up to eight, with terms unchanged, an automatic transfer, and claims routed by the date of the insured event: up to 31.12.2026 to הראל, from 1.1.2027 to איילון. That is the sourced set (press reports of Clalit's announcement: Calcalist, Globes, Maariv). The press disagree on the contract term (Calcalist: three years with options up to eight; Globes: eight years; TheMarker: 2027-2031), so state "from 1.1.2027" and not a length. Clalit's own LTC page, rendered on 8.11.2026, still presents the policy as "באמצעות ובאחריות הראל" for 1.1.2025 to 31.12.2026 and carries no 2027 notice, so the transfer remains press-sourced until Clalit publishes. Secondary summaries add that no new health declaration is needed, that premiums stay with Clalit and the members' fund, and that benefits already in payment stay with הראל; relay those only as "reported, confirm with the kupa". Never treat the new insurer name as a new or duplicate policy. The other kupot carry their own agreements (מכבי's page is branded "מכבי סיעודי - מנורה"); do not assume one insurer for all four.
  - In early October 2026 the regulator published, as a press notice rather than a formal document, a proposed new model: members under 55 to move to insurer-run personal long-term accumulation accounts chosen by tender, members 55 and over to stay as a closed group with rights preserved, and a state budget cushion for that group. It needs legislation, the Treasury's Budget Division opposes it, and the decision was left to the next government. It is a proposal. Do not describe it as in force, do not give it a date, and do not let a user cancel anything in anticipation of it.
- Do NOT assert that receiving גמלת סיעוד from ביטוח לאומי does or does not reduce a policy payout. The sources establish the two run in parallel; they do not settle the payout interaction. This is a question for the policy wording and the insurer.

What the regulator's own health-area FAQ adds on סיעוד (read 8.11.2026, cited in `evidence.json`):

- Stacking is the regulator's own position, not only the statute's: "אפשר להצטרף למספר פוליסות סיעוד במקביל ובכך להגדיל את גובה או תקופת התשלום". Two private סיעוד policies are LEGITIMATE STACKING; the question for the map is affordability, never duplication.
- Kupa policy plus an insurer's policy: "כן אך לא בהכרח", two separate claims, each tested on its own wording. One rejection does not decide the other.
- On a kupa switch "גם הביטוח הסיעודי דרך קופת החולים עובר לקופה החדשה באופן אוטומטי" per the regulator's FAQ; Clalit's page adds the condition: at registration the member must approve the transfer of entitlement data (checked through הר הביטוח), otherwise "לא יהיה ביטוח סיעודי ברצף" unless the previous insurer's certificate is sent "בתוך 180 ימים ממועד המעבר". So the move is automatic only once that box is ticked; confirm with the new kupa that it is active. The benefit and payment period are uniform across the kupot's group policies, and Clalit's premium table (correct for 1.11.2026) charges 0 for ages 0-17, so a child's kupa LTC line is never a cut candidate.
- **ערכי סילוק before any private סיעוד cancellation.** "בחלק מהפוליסות קיימת אפשרות לתבוע את חברת הביטוח ולקבל תשלום חודשי מופחת ("ערכי סילוק")", and the area's "תמונת מצב" page shows the policy type and whether it carries them. A policy with paid-up values is not worth zero after cancellation, which changes the arithmetic of a cut; one without them is lost entirely. Ask the insurer in writing either way.
- Group LTC continuity: a member who joined or renewed group LTC from 1.7.2004 must, after three consecutive years, be allowed to move "לפוליסת סיעוד אישית דומה, בלי לבדוק מחדש את המצב הרפואי". State the right as the FAQ states it; whether the insurer currently has an individual product to move into is a separate question, since individual סיעוד is no longer sold, so send the user to ask.
- The FAQ's generic trigger wording ("לא מסוגל לבצע לפחות 50% מהפעולות") predates the 1.1.2025 amendment and describes the older three-of-six test; it also notes that for policies joined after 2005 "תשישות נפש" qualifies. Quote the user's own policy, not the FAQ, for the trigger.


## אזור ביטוחי הבריאות, the health area of הר הביטוח (behind Step A)

The claim this skill carried through three versions, that הר הביטוח "holds no שב"ן data", came from the State Comptroller's 2020 report and was true of the main register, which lists only insurer-issued products. It is no longer the whole picture. In January 2023 the regulator announced, with מערך הדיגיטל הלאומי and משרד הבריאות, an upgraded health area that "יכלול לראשונה התייחסות לכיסוי הקיים בקופות החולים" and gives "פירוט על הכיסויים הביטוחיים בתחום הבריאות ועלותם והשוואה לכיסוי הקיים למבוטח בקופת החולים". The health-insurance circular (2022-1-14 as amended 7.2.2023) names "אזור ביטוחי הבריאות בהר הביטוח" as a permitted sales medium and obliges the marketer to show the candidate's שב"ן surgery cover "כפי שמופיע באתר הר הביטוח", which only works if the area carries it. The area lives at `hit.cma.gov.il` (header: "איזור ביטוחי בריאות"), behind the government login, and describes itself as answering "איזה ביטוחים יש לי היום ובאיזה חברות? מה גובה התשלום החודשי שלי? ... האם יש לי ביטוחים כפולים?".

What the site says about itself, read directly on its public pages on 8.11.2026 (home, about, the FAQ categories, the login landing page; the logged-in personal screens were NOT read, and nothing below describes them beyond what the FAQ says):

- **What it holds.** The home page: "ריכזנו עבורך את המידע על כיסויי הבריאות שלך, כולל מחיר חודשי. בנוסף, ניידע אותך אם יש לך מספר כיסויים כפולים." By default every health cover appears, "ממלכתי, שירותי בריאות נוספים של קופת החולים, ביטוח פרטי וקבוצתי". Kupa data refreshes daily, insurer data monthly. The about page names the creators as רשות שוק ההון and מערך הדיגיטל הלאומי (משרד הבריאות appears only in the January 2023 announcement).
- **What it does not hold.** "בשלב זה האתר מציג מידע אישי בלבד ללא בני משפחה נוספים": no children, no spouse, no ward. A policy may also be missing because the user signed a privacy form at purchase refusing data transfer to הר הביטוח ("אין לנו דרך לדעת האם מלאת טופס כזה בעבר"), because the monthly update has not caught a new policy, or because of a technical fault; the fallbacks the site itself names are the insurer's personal area, the pension clearing house, an agent, and the annual report the insurer must send.
- **Its two notices, in its own words.** 'כפל ביטוח' (FAQ "כתוב באתר שיש לי 'כפל ביטוח'. מה זה אומר?"): two or more covers for the same event. Its reasoning is the three-tier test: for "החזר על הוצאות (שיפוי)" the user "אפשר לקבל החזר רק עד גובה עלות הטיפול" (worked example: two 80% surgery-abroad policies pay 80,000 and then 16,000 on a 100,000 bill), and for "מענק (פיצוי)" the user gets "מ-2 הביטוחים את הסכום שמופיע בפוליסה" (two 3,000 personal-accident policies pay 6,000). Its own advice: "לפני שמבטלים את אחד הביטוחים חשוב לקרוא את תנאי הביטוחים האחרים ולוודא שהם בתוקף ומעניקים לך את הכיסוי הרצוי". The second term, 'חפיפה בכיסוי ביטוחי', is reserved for שב"ן against an insurer: "מצב שבו יש לך כיסוי דרך שירותי הבריאות הנוספים של קופת החולים וגם דרך חברת ביטוח המכסה מקרה דומה". Under 'כפל ביטוח' the FAQ repeats the cap, "ההחזר הכספי מכל חברות הביטוח יחד יהיה לכל היותר 100% מגובה ההוצאה הרפואית", and adds that for personal-accident פיצוי policies the chance of a claim and the sums are low, "לכן מומלץ לשקול את הצורך בתשלום על יותר מביטוח אחד מאותו הסוג".
- **Its payment typology** has three labels, not two: "החזר (שיפוי)", reimbursement against receipts, claimable from several policies "עד לקבלת סכום כולל השווה לסכום ההוצאה הרפואית"; "הסדר", a treatment with a provider from the insurer's list, approved by the insurer in advance, after which it "תסדיר את התשלום ישירות מול נותן השירות"; and "מענק (פיצוי)", a one-off pre-set sum paid "גם לא נגרמה לך הוצאה כספית", claimable from each policy separately. For the duplication test הסדר is שיפוי: the treatment is bought once.
- **Access.** Login is through מערכת ההזדהות הלאומית (login.gov.il): ID number and password (the route is named usernamePasswordSMSOtp, so a one-time SMS code follows), a biometric ID card or a smart card. The data is visible to the user alone; an agent or insurer representative sees it "רק אם נתת להם אישור לכך"; and in a sales conversation the seller "חייב להציע לך לעבור יחד איתו על ביטוחי הבריאות שלך כפי שהם מופיעים באתר", which is the circular's duty seen from the consumer side.
- **Other FAQ statements the skill relies on** (each cited in `evidence.json`): a private health policy cancelled and regretted within 60 days may be re-joined "באותם תנאים"; the insurer must execute a cancellation request within 15 days, and stopping payment without a request leaves the premium owed; basic covers (drugs, transplants, surgery abroad) must be held before Israeli surgery or consultation cover is bought, and the layers may sit with different insurers; policies joined before 2016 keep fixed terms while policies joined after 2016 are updated every two years; several סיעוד policies may be held in parallel "ובכך להגדיל את גובה או תקופת התשלום"; kupa LTC moves automatically on a kupa switch; kupa LTC plus an insurer's LTC means two separate claims; some LTC policies carry "ערכי סילוק", and the "תמונת מצב" page shows whether the user's does; a group LTC member since 1.7.2004 may after three years move to a similar individual policy without new underwriting (whether any insurer still sells one is a separate question); and the generic LTC trigger in the FAQ is still "לפחות 50% מהפעולות", wording that predates the 1.1.2025 group-policy amendment and must not be read as the group policies' current test.

An earlier consumer guide described the logged-in cards as carrying a red "חפיפה בין כיסויים" tag and a "החזר על הוצאות"/"פיצוי חד פעמי" tag. That is not the site's own vocabulary and the cards were not read, so the skill no longer quotes it; the notice wording to use is 'כפל ביטוח' and, for שב"ן, 'חפיפה בכיסוי ביטוחי'.

Limits: the area is an inventory aid. It does not read exclusions, waiting periods or sub-limits, it refreshes insurer data monthly, it shows one adult, and nothing in it tells the user which layer can be bought back. Use it to seed the map and to catch the שב"ן overlap the main register cannot show; keep the verdicts on the three-tier test and the safe-cut rule. The main register's own duplicate alert remains "אינה מחייבת תמיד כי נרכש כיסוי מיותר".

## Mortgage cover and the pupils' policy (behind Step E)

- **Mortgage cover is the most under-audited property line in the country, and Directive 451 hands the user three separate levers.** A bank may require the borrower to insure the property and to take life cover up to the loan amount, "ובלבד שהתאגיד הבנקאי יהא מוטב בלתי חוזר". But: (a) the bank must tell the borrower he may buy both directly rather than through the bank's own agency, and that notice must appear "באופן בולט ובאותיות גדולות" in the explanatory pages, in the loan agreement and in the annual notice, so a borrower paying the bank agency's premium is very often paying more than they need to; (b) the customer "יכול לבטל את הפוליסה בכל עת ללא עמלה, אם יציג בפני התאגיד הבנקאי פוליסת ביטוח חילופית שהתאגיד הבנקאי הוא המוטב הבלתי חוזר בה", which is the mechanism for switching without losing the loan; (c) the bank "לא יחייב לווה בביטוח הנכס המשועבד בערך העולה על יתרת ההלוואה המשוערכת הבלתי מסולקת", and must offer that lower-value option in a separate document. There is also no obligation to insure loans "עד 30,000 ש\"ח או שיתרתן אינה עולה על סכום זה, לרבות האפשרות לבטל ביטוחים קיימים אלה".
- **ביטוח מבנה taken with the mortgage against a separate ביטוח דירה is a ס' 59 question, not a health-style one.** Both insure one property against one risk, so if the sums together exceed the value of the property unreasonably, either the insured or an insurer may "לדרוש הפחתת סכומי הביטוח עד לשווי הנכס", with premiums falling correspondingly from the date of demand. Put it in REAL WASTE only after confirming both policies actually cover the structure rather than one covering only תכולה. When cancelling or reducing, the bank's charge has to be released: the borrower applies to the insurer to reduce the sum or cancel the charge, and the bank "ימסור ללווה אישור ייעודי, ממוען לחברת הביטוח" consenting. Never send a cancellation on a mortgage-linked policy before that confirmation exists, or the bank may treat the loan as in breach.
- **ביטוח תאונות אישיות לתלמידים is already paid for, and parents routinely buy it twice.** Every child entitled to free education "יהיה מבוטח בביטוח תאונות אישיות, באמצעות רשות החינוך המקומית", under a tendered contract, with the premium fixed nationally rather than by the school. It is not school-hours cover: the policy pays for injury "שאירע במקום כלשהו בשטח מדינת ישראל והשטחים המוחזקים, בכל שעה משעות היממה בין שיש לה קשר לפעילות המוסד החינוכי ובין אם לא". For 2026/27 (תקופת הביטוח 1.9.2026 - 31.8.2027, insurer איילון) the premium is unchanged at 69 ₪ with no deductible. A private child personal-accident rider bought on top is fixed-sum, so it does legitimately stack and is not unlawful, but the parent should be told plainly what the 69 ₪ already buys before deciding the rider is necessary.

## Critical illness and dental overlap (behind Step C)

- מחלות קשות: fixed sum, so it cannot duplicate anything, and no one should cancel it because an agent called it overlap. But "leave it alone" is not an audit. Households commonly hold this cover in four places at once: a standalone policy, a rider on a private health policy, an employer group benefit, and a rider inside a ביטוח מנהלים. Four premiums, and four covered-condition lists that differ materially in which conditions qualify and what survival period applies. The right output is not "stacks, keep everything" but "stacks, so compare the condition lists and the sums, and cut on price only where two lists are near-identical". That is a PARTIAL verdict, and it is the one place the skill applies PARTIAL to fixed-sum cover.
- שיניים: check it, because a large part of it may already be free. The public basket covers "טיפולי שיניים לילדים שטרם מלאו להם 18 שנים", and separately "טיפולי שיניים משמרים ומונעים למבוטחים שמלאו להם 72 שנים", with the periodic check-up, bitewing pair and scaling carried "בלא השתתפות עצמית". A dental policy bought for a nine-year-old, or for a grandparent over 72, is buying a lot of what the basket already provides. Dental benefits are normally paid as שיפוי, so the שב"ן-plus-policy overlap is a CAPPED verdict, not stacking. The real non-basket exception is אורתודנטיה, which the basket's own children's trauma item expressly carves out ("ולמעט אורתודנטיה"), so orthodontic cover belongs in PARTIAL rather than REAL WASTE.


## אכ"ע mechanics: the two 75% rules, the 100% offset and the 30% floor (behind Step D)

The two 75% rules are different instruments on different bases, and the skill must say which one it means:

- Inside a pension or provident fund: "סכום קצבת הנכות לא יעלה על 75% מן השכר המבוטח בקופת הגמל".
- In the uniform private policy: 75% of the average salary insured under that plan.

Cross-insurer offset does NOT trigger at 75%. It triggers only when total monthly benefit from all insurers exceeds "100% ממוצע השכר של המבוטח מכל מקור הכנסה", and even then the private policy still pays a floor: "הפיצוי החודשי שישולם למבוטח לא יפחת, בכל מקרה, משיעור של 30% מסכום הפיצוי החודשי לו הוא זכאי על פי תנאי התכנית". So the band above the per-policy cap and up to 100% of income is legitimate cover, not waste. A pension fund counts as a "מבטח אחר" for this purpose.

Excess-premium refund exists in the uniform policy but is capped at seven years AND conditional on a claim: "לא קרה מקרה הביטוח, המבוטח לא יהיה זכאי להחזר פרמיה". Never claim, never get it back. Tell the user this rather than letting them assume the money is banked.

Two related rules worth surfacing: at the point of sale the insurer must check the מסלקה, must refuse to sell against income that is already insured, and must cancel a duplicate (חוזר ביטוח 2018-1-8). And an אכ"ע premium may never be funded out of מרכיב הפיצויים.


## Travel cover and card benefits (behind Step F)

- Charging the trip to the card is generally NOT a condition on Israeli cards, unlike the US and UK model, and the belief that it is may be the most repeated false belief in Israeli consumer content here. Treat this as the default, not a universal: confirm it on the specific card, because one product conditioning on it would leave a user who relied on the rule with no cover. What matters is that the card is valid, and for discount benefits, that the policy premium is paid on that card.
- Most Israeli cards give a DISCOUNT, not embedded cover, typically a few free days and then a percentage off. Some premium tiers do embed cover and some issuers have discontinued it. This skill holds no sourced per-card list, so do not name products: send the user to their own card's benefit page.
- Everything that is not embedded requires activation before every single trip. The classic failure is believing the card covers you and never activating.
- The six-month pre-existing-condition exclusion is statutory, not underwriter discretion. It excludes "מחלה שבשלה היה המבוטח בטיפול או בהשגחה בעת צאתו לחוץ לארץ או במשך ששת החדשים שקדמו לצאתו".
- A standalone policy is genuinely needed on top of a card benefit for: pre-existing conditions, pregnancy, extreme and winter sports, trips longer than the age-banded day caps, ביטול נסיעה for non-medical reasons (absent from every card product examined), high-value gear above the כבודה sub-limits, and ages 80 and over.
- Do not invent per-card specifics. Give the user a checklist of what to verify on their own card's benefit page.
- Travel policies longer than three months are in scope for הר הביטוח; nothing written for three months or less is reported (חוזר 2021-1-3), so most trip policies will not be there.


## Statutory mechanics behind the three-tier table

The mechanics behind the table, which the agent should be able to state when challenged:

- ס' 59 is the ביטוח כפל provision, not ס' 56. Its own text is asset-bound: "בוטח נכס בפני סיכון אחד אצל יותר ממבטח אחד לתקופות חופפות". ס' 67 extends it to ביטוח אחריות, so liability cover is caught too, and ס' 64 makes it non-derogable except in the insured's favour.
- Under ס' 59(ג) the insurers are liable "יחד ולחוד לגבי סכום הביטוח החופף". The insured is not left chasing two companies; they simply cannot collect the overlap twice, and the insurers settle the split between themselves.
- ס' 54(ב) imports only ss. 42, 49, 52, 56, 61, 62 and 64 into loss-based accident, illness and disability cover. ס' 59 is absent from that list. What actually caps reimbursement health cover is ס' 56(א), "חובת השיפוי של המבטח תהיה כשיעור הנזק שנגרם".
- ס' 54(א) routes fixed-sum cover to פרק ב', which contains no כפל provision and no indemnity ceiling. So a standalone ריסק, a mortgage life policy and the שאירים cover inside the pension all pay on the same death. Say so plainly. A user who cancels one of them because an agent called it "duplication" has lost cover for nothing.


## ותק, אכשרה and cancellation mechanics (behind Steps G and H)

### Step G. The ותק and אכשרה trap, before anyone cancels anything

- Switching kupa preserves שב"ן ותק only on joining the new kupa's שב"ן within 90 days, and only between parallel tiers; moving up a tier means serving the receiving kupa's אכשרה for the upgrade.
- Cancelling and re-joining the SAME kupa restarts the waiting periods from scratch.
- Current tier names, because users and marketing material both garble them: מכבי sells two tiers to new joiners, מכבי זהב and מכבי שלי; מכבי כסף is a closed legacy tier ("תוכנית \"מכבי כסף\" נסגרה למצטרפים חדשים במאי 2012"), still held by members who joined before it closed in May 2012, and with an upgrade path out (to זהב or שלי) but no published route back in, so assume cancelling it is irreversible and confirm that with מכבי; כללית has מושלם זהב and מושלם פלטינום; מאוחדת has עדיף and שיא (there is no "זהב" at מאוחדת); לאומית has כסף and זהב. These are marketing names and they change, so confirm the current tier list on the kupa's own site before writing it into a report rather than treating this list as authoritative.

**Check residency before applying the safe-cut rule at all.** The rule assumes the kupa must re-accept the user. That fails for anyone whose entitlement under חוק ביטוח בריאות ממלכתי is unsettled, and for them the private policy may be the ONLY cover rather than the redundant layer. Stop and ask about status for a new oleh, a תושב חוזר, someone on relocation still holding Israeli policies, a dual citizen with foreign cover, or a non-resident spouse. A foreign employer's global plan alongside שב"ן is a real overlap worth mapping, but it is also the layer that disappears when the posting ends. This skill does not carry the waiting-period rules for any of these statuses: establish status, then have the user confirm entitlement before anything is cancelled.

### Step H. Cancellation mechanics

- The 14-day distance-selling withdrawal right does NOT apply to insurance. חוק הגנת הצרכן excludes insurers entirely. This is widely believed and false, and a user who relies on it will be caught out.
- The real instrument is חוזר ביטוח 2017-1-3, in force since 1.7.2017: every insurer must run a front-page area titled "ביטול פוליסה קיימת", plus email, personal-account, phone and fax routes, an automatic acknowledgement, and three business days to report deficiencies.
- Cancellation by the INSURED takes effect three days after the notice reaches the insurer: "מתבטל החוזה כעבור שלושה ימים מהיום שבו נמסרה הודעת הביטול למבטח". The fifteen days often quoted is the insurer's side: per the regulator's health-area FAQ, after a cancellation request "חברת הביטוח מחוייבת לפי החוק לבטל את הביטוח בתוך 15 ימים", and a user who merely stops paying without a request can be charged for the period. Do not swap the two.
- **A private health policy has a 60-day regret window after cancellation.** The same FAQ: "במידה וביקשת לבטל פוליסת בריאות פרטית והתחרטת במהלך 60 הימים הראשונים לביטול, באפשרותך לעדכן בכך את חברת הביטוח ולהצטרף מחדש באותם תנאים." That is the one genuinely reversible cut in this domain, which is why the cancellation letter should be dated and kept: the window is counted from the cancellation. It is stated for private health policies; do not extend it to סיעוד, life or group cover without the insurer's confirmation.
- Before cancelling, the FAQ's own checklist: the financial exposure if the event happens uninsured; whether another cover already gives the same reimbursement or service; that any new application is re-priced on current age and health; and, where the cancellation is because a new policy was bought, "אל תבטל אותו עד שלא תקבל אישור מחברת הביטוח החדשה שצורפת לפוליסה". When moving between insurers a waiting period may mean paying both for a while.
- Life insurance may be cancelled at any time by written notice.
- שב"ן cancellation notice is set by each kupa's תקנון, not by statute. Check the specific kupa.
- **Individual health policies renew on a two-year cycle, and the renewal is the audit's natural moment.** Under חוזר ביטוח 2015-1-19 (עריכת תכנית לביטוח בריאות פרט) a policy sold from 1.2.2016 is renewed every two years; the insurer must send the renewal notice 60 to 120 days before the renewal date, stating the date and any change in cover or premium. If the policy was renewed without the insured's explicit consent and the insured notifies the insurer within 60 days after the renewal date, "תבוטל הפוליסה לגביו במועד חידוש הביטוח ויושבו למבוטח דמי הביטוח שנגבו בשל תקופה זו", provided no claim was filed for an event in those 60 days. So a user who finds a duplicate just after a renewal letter may unwind that renewal to its date rather than from three days after notice. Group policies are outside this circular.
- An insurer may not sell a duplicate פיצוי policy unless the insured confirms in writing after being told they will be charged for both (חוזר צירוף 2016-1-7, as amended through 2023-1-5). And a הר הביטוח lookup is required before every sale, with no opt-out at all in שיווק יזום.

## The שקל ראשון reform (behind Step C)

**Two dates, two mechanisms.** The health-insurance reform itself (חוזר ביטוח 2022-1-14 as amended by 2023-1-2) took effect for new individual sales on **1 October 2023**, a date the regulator moved from 1.5.2023 to align with section 53 of the 2023-2024 Economic Arrangements Law. It restructured individual health cover into tiers: the first is a "פוליסת בריאות בסיסית" (surgery and surgery alternatives abroad, drugs outside the basket), and Israeli surgery is a separate tier sold in three forms, שקל ראשון, משלים שב"ן without deductible, and "משלים שב"ן עם השתתפות עצמית של 5,000 ש"ח", which the marketer must present first. Where the candidate asks about extending Israeli surgery cover, the marketer must show "הכיסוי לניתוחים... במסגרת השב"ן של קופת החולים, כפי שמופיע באתר הר הביטוח" and explain what the extension adds. A policy bought under these rules by a שב"ן member is משלים שב"ן by default, so post-October-2023 individual buyers are usually not the duplicating population; pre-2016 buyers, returners and group members are.

The second mechanism is the automatic transfer, legislated in the Arrangements Law: on 1 June 2024 holders of שקל ראשון individual policies bought between February 2016 and 30 September 2023 who were also שב"ן members were moved to משלים שב"ן unless they opted out. Calcalist reported in May 2025, from Treasury data, that of about 1.65 million שקל ראשון individual policyholders about 636,000 were transferred (the rest had bought before February 2016), and that about 42% of those transferred went back to שקל ראשון within the reversal year; the Treasury's own estimate was an average excess payment of about 1,300 ₪ a year per שקל ראשון holder. Treat those figures as press-reported, not official. Their consequence for the audit is simple: the population still paying twice is large, and it is made of pre-2016 buyers, returners and group members.

Handle the reform correctly, because most secondary sources do not. It took effect in **June 2024**; February 2016 is only the purchase-date cutoff defining which policies were caught, so never call it a 2016 event. It applied to פוליסות פרט only, and the reversal window ran to 31 May 2025, a year from the transfer, though some insurers extended their own, so check rather than assume it is closed. **The GROUP market was affected far less and with delay**, because group policies update only every few years under the policyholder-insurer agreement, so much group surgery cover is still מהשקל הראשון. Do NOT say group policies were never converted; have the user check their own. Anyone who bought individually before February 2016 was not caught either. Pre-2016 buyers, returners and group members are this skill's core audience, so ask explicitly whether the policy is individual or group and when it was bought, and never write the health section as though the reform solved the problem. Group cover also has a five-year structural limit and a conversion right on leaving, at a much higher individual premium.


## Contents riders and service contracts (behind Step E)

- תכולה riders are PARTIAL, not waste. The standard policy caps jewellery and excludes theft outside the home; a כל הסיכונים rider lifts exactly those limits. Ask what it covers before calling it redundant.
- כתבי שירות are the highest-yield manual audit here. Towing, home trades and windscreen typically sit in three or four places at once: a policy rider, a card benefit, a direct subscription, an employer or utility bundle. Service contracts typically carry a clawback and a no-double-benefits term, so the user gets the tow once and the extras deliver only monthly charges. Start from הר הביטוח rather than around it: it lists the insurer-issued כתבי שירות alongside the policies, with the premium paid for each ("כל פוליסות הביטוח וכתבי השירות בחברות הביטוח השונות כולל הפרמיה המשולמת במקום אחד"), and its own definition of a product is "פוליסת ביטוח או כתב שירות". What it does not carry is the non-insurance layer, which is not an insurance product at all: standalone service-company subscriptions, card benefits, and employer or utility bundles. Those come from the card and bank statements.
