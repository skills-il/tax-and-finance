# Israeli Cashback and Deals Platforms Guide

## Cashback Platforms

### Cashback.co.il
- **Type:** Browser extension + mobile app
- **How it works:** Click through their link or use extension before purchasing. Cashback is tracked automatically.
- **Payout:** Monthly to a bank account or PayPal, above a minimum balance. Read the current threshold on the platform, it changes
- **Coverage:** several hundred Israeli and international stores; check the live store list before promising a rate
- **Typical cashback:** 2-8%

### Cashdo
- **Type:** Browser extension + mobile app
- **How it works:** Similar to Cashback.co.il. Install extension, shop normally.
- **Payout:** To a bank account, above a minimum balance. Check the current threshold on the platform
- **Coverage:** several hundred Israeli retailers; the partner list moves, so verify the specific store before routing a purchase
- **Typical cashback:** 2-10%

### PayBack
- **Type:** Cashback service
- **How it works:** Earn cash back ("kesef chozer") on purchases at partner stores
- **Payout:** Rebated to the user across partner stores
- **Coverage:** Multiple retail chains

## Deals Platforms

### BuyMe
- **Type:** Gift card marketplace
- **Specialty:** Gift cards for a large brand catalogue, often at a discount
- **Best for:** Buying gift cards below face value (5-15% off) as a savings strategy
- **Important:** A card bought online is a distance sale, so it can be cancelled within 14 days under Consumer Protection Law s.14ג(ג), with a refund inside 14 days and a cancellation fee of at most 5% of the price or 100 NIS, whichever is lower (s.14ה(ב)(1)). A card bought in a shop generally cannot be returned. Either way the card must run at least 5 years from issuance (Consumer Protection Law, sec. 14ח(ב)), and once expired they can be renewed on request for a further 5 years, or 10 years where the balance exceeds 150 NIS, under the Payment Services (Exemption) Regulations. Use is blocked from the date of a bankruptcy or liquidation order against the issuer.
- **Tip:** Buy gift cards for stores where you regularly shop

### Yours.co.il
- **Type:** Members' discount club
- **Specialty:** Benefits across food, leisure, shopping, events, and travel
- **Best for:** Members looking for general everyday discounts

## Tips for Maximum Savings

1. **Never use two cashback extensions simultaneously** -- they will conflict and neither will track
2. **Clear cookies before clicking cashback links** -- ensures proper attribution
3. **Screenshot your cashback tracking** -- in case of disputes
4. **Check cashback rates before and during sales** -- some platforms reduce rates during Black Friday
5. **Combine cashback with credit card benefits** -- these usually stack, but not always. Platform cashback is normally computed on the post-discount net amount, and purchases paid with gift cards or store credit are routinely excluded from tracking altogether. Check the platform's terms for the specific store before promising the user a combined rate
