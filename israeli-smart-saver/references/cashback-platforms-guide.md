# Israeli Cashback and Deals Platforms Guide

## Cashback Platforms

### Cashback.co.il
- **Type:** Browser extension + mobile app
- **How it works:** Click through their link or use extension before purchasing. Cashback is tracked automatically.
- **Payout:** Monthly to a bank account or PayPal, above a minimum balance. Read the current threshold on the platform, it changes
- **Coverage:** Israeli and international stores; check the live store list before promising a rate
- **Rebate:** set per store and changes often; read the live rate

### Cashdo
- **Type:** Browser extension + mobile app
- **How it works:** Similar to Cashback.co.il. Install extension, shop normally.
- **Payout:** To a bank account, above a minimum balance. Check the current threshold on the platform
- **Coverage:** Israeli retailers; the partner list moves, so verify the specific store before routing a purchase
- **Rebate:** set per store; read the live rate

### PayBack
- **Type:** Max's own cashback service (the site is titled "PayBack של max")
- **How it works:** Earn cash back ("kesef chozer") on purchases at partner stores
- **Payout:** Rebated to the user across partner stores
- **Coverage:** Multiple retail chains
- **Note:** Because PayBack belongs to Max, check its terms before assuming its rebate stacks with a Max card's own cashback

## Deals Platforms

### BuyMe
- **Type:** Gift card marketplace
- **Specialty:** Gift cards and experience vouchers for a large brand catalogue
- **Best for:** Gifts; in general experience a discount, where there is one, comes through an employer welfare club or a card club offer. A single-experience voucher may fall outside the gift-card rules of s.14ח, whose definition excludes a card meant for one type of product or service only
- **Important:** A card bought online is a distance sale, so it can be cancelled within 14 days under Consumer Protection Law s.14ג(ג), with a refund inside 14 days and a cancellation fee of at most 5% of the price or 100 NIS, whichever is lower (s.14ה(ב)(1)). Whether a card bought in a shop can be returned depends on the shop's posted returns policy (s.4ג). A general gift card with an expiry date must run at least 5 years from issuance (s.14ח(ב)); the renewal right (a further 5 years, or 10 years where the balance exceeds 150 NIS) under the Payment Services (Exemption) Regulations applies only to exempt multi-merchant cards up to 1,500 NIS, not reloadable, issued from 14 April 2022, and those regulations run until 14 July 2028 (a voucher for one specific product or service with no amount printed on it has a two-year floor where more than 50 NIS was paid, and no renewal right). Use is blocked from the date of a bankruptcy or liquidation order against the issuer. See `consumer-law-details.md`.
- **Tip:** Buy gift cards for stores where you regularly shop

### Employer and union welfare clubs
- **Type:** Closed members' clubs run for a workplace, union or sector (מועדון רווחה / ועד עובדים)
- **Specialty:** Discounted gift cards, leisure and shopping benefits
- **Best for:** Anyone whose workplace has one. Yours.co.il, for example, is the welfare club for local-authority employees, not a club open to the public

## Tips for Maximum Savings

1. **Never use two cashback extensions simultaneously** -- in general experience they conflict and neither tracks
2. **Clear cookies before clicking cashback links** -- a common tip for clean attribution
3. **Screenshot your cashback tracking** -- in case of disputes
4. **Check cashback rates before and during sales** -- rates move during big sale events, in either direction
5. **Combine cashback with credit card benefits** -- these usually stack, but not always. Platform cashback is normally computed on the post-discount net amount, and purchases paid with gift cards or store credit are routinely excluded from tracking altogether. Check the platform's terms for the specific store before promising the user a combined rate
