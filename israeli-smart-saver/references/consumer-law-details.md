# Consumer-law details behind the Gotchas

Primary texts: Consumer Protection Law 5741-1981 (Nevo consolidated text, current to 30-09-2026) and the Payment Services (Exemption) Regulations 5782-2022 (Nevo, current to 27-05-2026). This is general information, not legal advice; check the contract and the current text before relying on any of it.

## A. Gift cards and vouchers (Gotcha 2)

**Bought online.** A card bought online is a distance sale. It can be cancelled from the deal until 14 days after the later of receiving the card and receiving the s.14ג(ב) disclosure document (s.14ג(ג)). On a cancellation that is not for a defect or breach, the business refunds within 14 days and may keep at most 5% of the price or 100 NIS, whichever is lower (s.14ה(ב)(1)); where the cancellation is for a defect, a mismatch with the disclosed details, late or non-delivery, or another breach, it may keep nothing (s.14ה(א)). The s.14ג(ד) exclusions (perishables, information, custom-made goods, opened recordable media, short-notice travel and leisure) do not name a gift card. A person aged 65 or over, a person with a disability, or a new immigrant within five years has four months where the deal involved a conversation with the business, including an electronic one (s.14ג1).

**Bought in a shop.** Section 4ג requires a shop to post its returns policy for goods returned without a defect; where it posts none, the policy is presumed to allow returns. Whether a gift card counts as goods for this purpose is not settled in the texts read, so tell the user to check the posted policy rather than asserting either answer.

**Consumer Protection Law s.14ח.** A "תו קנייה" is a card or document bought for money that lets the holder buy goods or services from a business up to its face value, excluding one meant for one particular type of product or service only (s.14ח(א)). An issuer that limits validity must print the issue and expiry dates, and validity may not be less than five years from issue (s.14ח(ב)). From the date of a bankruptcy or liquidation order against the issuer, the holder can no longer buy with the card.

**Payment Services (Exemption) Regulations.** They cover an exempt payment instrument: not intended for a specific payer, holding at most 1,500 NIS, not reloadable, presented and paid separately from other instruments, and charged immediately against the balance (reg 2(א)(1)). Regulations 2 and 3 apply to instruments issued from 14 April 2022 (reg 4(ב)) and are in force until 14 July 2028 (reg 5). For such an instrument the provider:

- may not limit validity to less than five years; for a voucher for one specific product or service with no amount printed on it, not less than two years, and only where more than 50 NIS was paid for it (reg 3(א)(4)(א));
- must renew an expired instrument at the holder's request for a further five years, or ten years where the balance exceeds 150 NIS; the specific-product voucher has no renewal right (reg 3(א)(4)(ב)-(ג));
- must notify the holder at least once a year, a month before expiry, and a month before the renewal window closes, where the balance exceeds 50 NIS; for an instrument issued before the 2026 amendment, only where it exceeds 100 NIS (reg 3(א)(4)(ד), reg 4(ג));
- where a merchant cancels a purchase that was paid WITH the instrument, must allow the refund back onto the instrument as a balance (reg 3(א)(5)). This is about a purchase made with the card. Cancelling the purchase of the card itself is a s.14ה cancellation, with the price refunded.

## B. Fixed-term and promotional deals (Gotcha 4)

A "עסקה לתקופה קצובה" is (1) a deal for a set period, (2) a deal in which, for a set period, the price is reduced or another benefit is given, whether the deal itself is fixed or open-ended, excluding a price the business cuts for a limited period without any condition, or (3) linked deals whose prices affect each other for a set period (s.13א(א)). The notice period runs from 60 days to 30 days before the end date.

Where the business charges by standing authorisation it must state the end date in the notice period, in the contract or s.14ג(ב) document, on every invoice, receipt or payment notice in the last three months (at most once a month, with special prominence), and by SMS 21 days before if the consumer gave a mobile number (s.13א(ב)).

A term continuing the deal past the end date has no effect (s.13א(ג)(1)). An extension the consumer agrees to in the notice period runs as agreed; one agreed after the notice period cannot worsen the terms in its first 30 days, and the consumer may withdraw (s.13א(ג)(2)). The burden of proving consent is on the business (s.13א(ה)). The two exceptions (s.13א(ד)) are independent:

- **(ד)(1)**: goods or services in the Third Schedule (telephony as defined in the Communications Law, and gas supply) may continue if a written notice in the notice period gave the end date and the terms, and said the deal continues unless the consumer ends it and that the consumer may end it at any time.
- **(ד)(2)**: a discount-period or linked deal may continue if the post-end amounts were stated clearly and in detail in a written document at contracting, and a written notice with the terms was given in the notice period. No notice is needed where the end date falls no later than four months after contracting; the consumer may then end the deal after that date with no charge.

Statutory damages under s.31א(א)(2א) require a prior written request (s.31א(ב)).

## C. Gym and timeshare (Gotcha 6)

Section 13א1 lets a consumer cancel at any time a fixed-term deal of a type in the Fourth Schedule.

**Gym (מכון כושר), Fourth Schedule item 2.** Written notice; effective at the end of one month from delivery. If performance has begun, the business may keep only: (1) the proportional part of the consideration for use up to the effective date; and (2) a cancellation fee equal to the number of months from the start of the deal to the effective date multiplied by the difference between the monthly price in the deal (the price for the fixed term divided by its months) and the monthly price on the alternative open-ended track, only where that alternative price was disclosed in writing before contracting. The fee may not exceed the lower of 25% of the consideration if cancelled in the first third of the term, 20% in the second third, 17% in the last third, or the amount the consumer would have paid for the rest of the term. "Consideration" includes registration fees. The definition excludes a curriculum-based course with advance-only enrolment that cannot be joined later, and a pool-only venue operating five months a year or less.

**Timeshare (יחידת נופש), item 1.** Written notice; effective at the end of the annual usage period after the notice. No cancellation fee; if performance has begun, the business may keep the proportional price for use up to the effective date and 50% of the remainder, the latter only if it did not restrict the consumer's right to assign the contract.
