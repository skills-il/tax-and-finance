# Seasonal Sales Calendar (Israel)

| Event | When | What to Buy | Discounts Often Seen |
|-------|------|-------------|-----------------|
| Post-Purim sales | March | Costumes, candy (clearance) | 50-70% |
| Pesach sales | March-April | Kitchen items, home goods | 20-40% |
| End of summer | August-September | Summer clothes, AC units | 30-50% |
| Black Friday (Israel) | November | Electronics, fashion, online shopping | 20-50% |
| Hannukah deals | December | Toys, gift items | 10-30% |
| End of year | December | Fashion clearance, annual subscriptions | 20-50% |
| Tu BiShvat | January-February | Plants, garden items | 10-20% |
