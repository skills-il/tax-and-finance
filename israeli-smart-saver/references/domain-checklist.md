## 1. Must cover (core)

### A. Classifying the contract (the gate before any cancellation advice)

1. **Four distinct exit regimes, classified first**: continuous deal (s.13ד + s.14ט), fixed-term deal end-of-term rules (s.13א), Fourth Schedule fixed-term deals (s.13א1), distance/door-to-door cooling-off (s.14ג / s.14), plus credit-voucher rules (s.14ז). Source: CPL ss.13א, 13א1, 13ד, 14, 14ג, 14ז [STAT].
2. **Definition of "עסקה מתמשכת"**: purchase of goods or services on an ongoing basis, fixed-term or open-ended, including any change or addition that is not a new deal; gas supply excluded from this definition. Source: s.13ג(א) [STAT].
3. **Definition of "עסקה לתקופה קצובה", three limbs**: (1) deal for a set period; (2) a set period of reduced price or other benefit, whether the deal itself is fixed or open-ended; (3) linked deals whose price affects each other for a set period. Carve-out: an unconditional, limited-time price cut the business makes during the deal is NOT a fixed-term deal. Source: s.13א(א) [STAT].

### B. Fixed-term and promotional-price deals (s.13א)

4. **Notice period** = from 60 days to 30 days before the end date. Source: s.13א(א) [STAT].
5. **Notice duty** where the business charges by standing authorisation (הרשאה לחיוב): notify the end date within the notice period; also state it in the contract or s.14ג(ב) document; on every invoice/receipt/payment notice in the last three months (once a month max), with special prominence; and by SMS 21 days before the end date if a mobile number was given (next day if Shabbat/holiday). Source: s.13א(ב)(1)-(3) [STAT].
6. **Default rule**: a contract term continuing the deal past the end date has no effect; the contract is treated as ending on that date. Source: s.13א(ג)(1) [STAT].
7. **Extension by consent**: offer in the notice period plus consumer consent extends as agreed; if the offer is made after the notice period, for the first 30 days of the extension the business may not raise the price or worsen terms and the consumer may withdraw consent. Burden of proving consent is on the business. Source: s.13א(ג)(2), 13א(ה) [STAT].
8. **Third Schedule exception (telephony, gas supply)**: may continue past the end date only if written notice in the notice period states the end date, the terms, that the deal continues unless the consumer ends it, and that the consumer may end it at any time. Third Schedule lists only "שירות טלפוניה כהגדרתו בחוק התקשורת" and gas supply (item 3 deleted); home internet and pay-TV promotions are NOT in it and fall under the default rule or the discount-period exception. Source: s.13א(ד)(1), Third Schedule [STAT].
9. **Discount-period / linked-deal exception**: may continue at the post-promotion price only if (a) the post-end amounts were stated clearly and in detail in a written document at contracting (may be CPI-linked; if set by government or a minister, the notice states the current price and that it is so set), and (b) a written notice with the terms was given in the notice period. No notice needed where the end date falls within four months of contracting, and then the consumer may end the deal after the end date at no charge. Source: s.13א(ד)(2)(א)-(ב) [STAT].
10. **Remedy**: continuing to charge after the end date contrary to 13א(ג), or without the written 13א(ד) notice, is a 31א exemplary-damages ground. Source: s.31א(א)(2א) [STAT].

### C. Cancelling a continuous deal (ss.14ט, 13ד, 13ד1)

11. **Six cancellation channels the business must accept**: orally by phone or at the premises (unless law requires written notice); registered mail; email; fax if the business has one; internet where the deal can be made online; any means the Minister sets. Source: s.14ט(א) [STAT].
12. **Dedicated cancellation link** on the website home page, positioned prominently and clearly, for any deal that can be made online. Source: s.14ט(ב) [STAT].
13. **Content of the notice**: name and ID number; for an oral notice, an additional identifier if one was agreed at contracting. Source: s.14ט(ג) [STAT].
14. **Business's disclosure duty**: the cancellation channels, contact details for each, and required notice content, in writing no later than supply; repeated on invoices/receipts/payment notices, in the end-of-term notice, on the website home page and next to the cancellation link, adjacent and prominent. Source: s.14ט(ד)-(ו) [STAT].
15. **Effective date**: contract ends within 3 business days of the 14ט notice, or 6 business days from posting if by registered mail, unless the consumer named a later date; the business must stop supply and stop charging for anything after that date. Source: s.13ד(ג) [STAT].
16. **Force-majeure carve-out**: where continued charging results from circumstances the business did not and could not foresee and cancellation was impossible, 13ד(ג) and 31א(א)(2ב) do not apply while those circumstances last. Source: s.13ד(ד) [STAT].
17. **Overcharge on a standing-authorisation deal**: investigate within 10 business days; refund within 4 business days with shekel interest from collection plus 16 NIS for expenses (refund under 50 NIS may be credited on the next bill if that notice goes out within 30 days); reasoned written answer within 21 business days. The 16 NIS and 50 NIS are base amounts updated by CPI every 1 January since 2013, so the current figures are higher [VERIFY current indexed amounts]. Source: s.13ד1(א)-(ג) [STAT].
18. **Exemplary damages (31א)**: up to 10,000 NIS without proof of loss for listed breaches, including continued charging after a 14ט cancellation (para (2ב)), failure to refund an overcharge under 13ד1 (para (2ב1)), 13א breaches (para (2א)), non-refund after distance-sale cancellation (para (4)), and door-to-door non-refund (para (3)). Precondition: a prior written request, including electronic; for (2ב) a 14ט notice suffices except an oral one under 14ט(א)(1). Court may award up to 50,000 NIS for a repeated or continuing breach or aggravated circumstances. Source: s.31א(א), (ב), (ג) [STAT].
19. **Free phone line with a human, for cancellation**: businesses in the Second Schedule (mobile and fixed telephony licensees, internet access, cable and satellite TV, gas suppliers, electricity essential-service suppliers, water suppliers) must provide a free phone service with a human answer; where the phone service includes an automatic call-routing system, for fault handling, billing enquiries and "סיום התקשרות" the wait for a professional human answer may not exceed six minutes from the start of the call, and the caller may not be diverted to voicemail unless they choose it; a sector regulator may approve deviations from the six minutes (s.18ב(א1)(4)). Source: s.18ב(א), (א1), Second Schedule [STAT].

### D. Fourth Schedule fixed-term deals (s.13א1), one item per row

20. **General rule**: a consumer may cancel at any time a fixed-term deal of a type in the Fourth Schedule, in the manner and on the conditions set there; "fixed-term deal" here includes purchase of an asset; this is in addition to ss.14, 14א, 14ג, 14ג1 and 14ו. Source: s.13א1(א), (ג) [STAT].
21. **Fourth Schedule definition**: "דמי ביטול" = any payment demanded because of cancellation, whatever it is called. Source: Fourth Schedule preamble [STAT].
22. **Row 2, gym (מכון כושר), manner and timing**: written cancellation notice; takes effect at the end of one month from delivery. Source: Fourth Schedule item 2 [STAT].
23. **Row 2, gym, what the business may keep**: refund or cancel the charge, except, if performance began: (1) the proportional part of the consideration for the right of use up to the effective date; (2) a cancellation fee = number of months from start to effective date multiplied by the difference between the monthly price in the deal and the monthly price on the alternative (open-ended) track, and only if that alternative-track price was disclosed in writing before contracting. Source: Fourth Schedule item 2(א) [STAT].
24. **Row 2, gym, cap on the cancellation fee**: the lower of (1) 25% of the consideration if cancelled in the first third of the term, 20% in the second third, 17% in the last third; or (2) the amount the consumer would have paid for the rest of the term from the effective date. "Consideration" includes registration fees. Source: Fourth Schedule item 2(ב) and definitions [STAT].
25. **Row 2, gym, definition and exclusions**: a gym under the Gyms (Licensing and Supervision) Law 5754-1994 plus any sports or health-club venue (pool, exercise and body-shaping classes, sauna, jacuzzi), EXCLUDING a curriculum-based class with advance-only enrolment that cannot be joined later, and a pool-only venue operating five months a year or less. Source: Fourth Schedule item 2 definitions [STAT].
26. **Row 1, timeshare (יחידת נופש), manner and timing**: written notice; takes effect at the end of the annual usage period following the notice (or the current one if given during it). Source: Fourth Schedule item 1 [STAT].
27. **Row 1, timeshare, permitted retention**: no cancellation fee, except if performance began: (1) the proportional price for usage up to the effective date; (2) 50% of the remaining price after (1), only if the business did not restrict the consumer's right to assign the contract; sums for the period after the effective date refunded with linkage differentials. Source: Fourth Schedule item 1(א)-(ב) [STAT]. (Included because the skill already references timeshare in its gym gotcha.)

### E. Distance and door-to-door purchases (ss.14, 14ג, 14ג1, 14ה)

28. **Distance-sale definition and disclosures**: a deal made after "שיווק מרחוק" (post, phone, radio, TV, any electronic communication, fax, catalogue, ads) without joint presence; pre-contract disclosures (7 items) and a written document no later than supply (6 items incl. manufacturer and country of origin). Source: s.14ג(א), (ב), (ו) [STAT].
29. **Cancellation window, goods**: from the deal until 14 days after the later of receiving the goods or the 14ג(ב) document. Source: s.14ג(ג)(1) [STAT].
30. **Cancellation window, services**: 14 days from the later of the deal or the document; for a continuous deal, whether or not service has started; for a non-continuous deal, only if at least two non-rest days before the service date. Source: s.14ג(ג)(2) [STAT].
31. **Distance-sale exclusions**: perishables; lodging, travel, holiday or leisure services if cancellation falls within seven non-rest days before the service; "information" under the Computers Law; goods custom-made for the consumer; recordable or copyable goods whose original packaging was opened. Source: s.14ג(ד) [STAT].
32. **Refund on cancellation without fault**: within 14 days of the notice; cancel the charge and give a copy of the cancellation; cancellation fee at most 5% of the price or 100 NIS, whichever is lower; consumer returns goods to the place of business. Source: s.14ה(ב) [STAT].
33. **Refund on cancellation for defect, mismatch, late supply or other breach**: within 14 days, NO cancellation fee at all; consumer makes the goods available where they were delivered. Source: s.14ה(א) [STAT].
34. **Ancillary charges on cancellation**: proportional payment for a continuous service already given; installation in the home up to 100 NIS; "cancellation fee" includes shipping, packing and any claimed expense. Source: s.14ה(ב1), (ב2), (ד) [STAT].
35. **Senior citizens (65+), people with disabilities, new immigrants (within 5 years)**: four months to cancel a door-to-door deal, and a distance deal if contracting included a conversation (including an electronic one); the business may demand only one listed certificate (Fifth Schedule list). Source: s.14ג1(א)-(ד), Fifth Schedule [STAT].
36. **Door-to-door sale (רוכלות)**: 14 days for goods (from the later of delivery or the prescribed details) and for services (continuous: whether or not started; non-continuous: only if not started); refund of consideration; installation up to 100 NIS; perishables excluded. Source: s.14(א)-(ג) [STAT].

### F. Gift cards, credit vouchers and the Payment Services exemption

37. **Credit voucher (שובר זיכוי) after cancellation without a refund right**: indelible; face value = amount paid; valid at least two years from handover; no invoice required to redeem; any restricting condition (including during a special sale) is void, subject to ministerial exceptions; validity and the "no restrictions" statement printed prominently. Source: s.14ז(א), (ב) [STAT].
38. **Cash change on a voucher**: if the purchase is cheaper than the voucher and the difference is not more than 5% of the face value or 100 NIS, whichever is lower, the consumer gets the difference in cash. Source: s.14ז(ג) [STAT].
39. **Gift voucher (שובר מתנה) gets the same s.14ז rules**. Source: s.14ז(ד) [STAT]. (The current skill frames 14ז as refund-voucher only.)
40. **"תו קנייה" definition and its exclusion**: a card or document bought for money allowing purchase from a business up to its face value, EXCLUDING one intended only for a specific type of product or service. Source: s.14ח(א) [STAT].
41. **Gift-card validity floor**: an issuer that limits validity must print its name, issue date and expiry date clearly; validity not less than five years from issue; after a bankruptcy or liquidation order against the issuer, the holder cannot buy with it from the date of the order. Source: s.14ח(ב) [STAT].
42. **Uniform face value across all listed merchants; change given as a credit voucher usable at every branch** (may be limited at a branch under a different trade name); a voucher for returning an item bought with a gift card may carry the gift card's own restrictions. Source: s.14ח(ג)-(ה) [STAT].
43. **Payment-instrument exemption conditions (closed gift cards)**: not intended for a specific payer; maximum stored value 1,500 NIS; not reloadable; shown and used separately from other instruments; charge is immediate against the balance with no deferred payments. Second track: a reloadable card up to 1,500 NIS handed out by a government body or non-profit as economic aid, usable only physically at a limited set of suppliers. Source: Payment Services (Exemption) Regs reg 2(א)(1)-(2) [REG].
44. **Issuer duties for such cards**: disclose issuer identity, how to redeem, list of merchants, validity, lost/stolen rules; restore the balance of a lost or stolen card and freeze it at the apparent holder's request where the card can be identified with reasonable effort (not paper vouchers); uniform value across merchants. Source: reg 3(א)(1)-(3) [REG].
45. **Validity and renewal**: validity not less than five years; a voucher for a specific product or service with no stated amount, at least two years if more than 50 NIS was paid. On request, an expired instrument is renewed for a further five years from expiry, or ten years if the stored balance exceeds 150 NIS; renewal must be simple and convenient; product-specific vouchers are not renewable. Source: reg 3(א)(4)(א)-(ג) [REG].
46. **Reminder notices**: where the balance exceeds 50 NIS, the issuer notifies the apparent holder (if locatable with reasonable effort) at least once a year, plus one month before expiry and one month before the end of the renewal window. For instruments issued before publication of the 2026 amending regulations, the notice duty applies only where the balance exceeds 100 NIS. Source: reg 3(א)(4)(ד), reg 4(ג) [REG].
47. **Refund after consumer cancellation is credited as a balance on the same instrument** (not paper-based instruments). Source: reg 3(א)(5), reg 2(ב)(3) [REG].
48. **Dates**: regs 2-3 apply to instruments issued from 14 April 2022, and are in force until 14 July 2028; product-specific vouchers issued before the 2026 amendment keep the earlier rules. The skill must not present the renewal right as permanent law. Source: reg 4(ב), 4(ד), reg 5 [REG].

### G. Bank of Israel household fee reform (circular ח-06-2851), one item per row

49. **Debit-card monthly fee cap**: "דמי כרטיס חיוב מיידי" not above 7 NIS (market average was about 9 NIS), by price-control order, in force 1 October 2026, applying to all cards including those issued before. Source: circular paras 9-10, 29, 36ב(1) [BOI].
50. **Old debit-card exemption runs in parallel**: the existing three-year exemption for a customer who also holds a credit card continues until 1 July 2027; banks may end it earlier only by implementing early. Source: circular para 29 [BOI].
51. **Fee abolished from 1 October 2026, drawer charged on a cheque returned for a technical reason** ("חיוב מושך בהחזרת שיק מסיבה טכנית"). Source: paras 22, 36ב(2) [BOI].
52. **Fee abolished from 1 October 2026, locating accounts ("איתור חשבונות")**. Source: paras 26, 36ב(2) [BOI].
53. **Fee abolished from 1 October 2026, handling inheritances and estates ("טיפול בירושות ועיזבונות")**. Source: paras 30, 36ב(2) [BOI].
54. **"ניהול חשבון תשלום" base fee from 1 July 2027**: at most 10 NIS a month for the first 100 included actions. Source: paras 5, 36א [BOI].
55. **Low-activity tier**: at most 5 NIS where 0 to 2 actions were made that month (the explanatory annex says "עד 2"). Source: para 5, Annex text [BOI].
56. **Per-action charge above 100**: at most 1 NIS for each included action beyond 100. Source: para 5 [BOI].
57. **Dormant accounts ("חשבונות ללא תנועה" under the Banking Ordinance)**: no account-management fee. Source: regulatory report section on the new model [BOI].
58. **Scope and enrolment**: individuals and small businesses with an active payment account; enrolment automatic, no opt-in; charged once a month (not necessarily calendar month); banks may start before 1 July 2027. Replaces the 2014 track service (NBT directive 423 cancelled when the rules take effect). Source: paras 4, 6, 11, 34, 36א [BOI].
59. **Services included in the flat fee (Annex F)**: cash deposit, withdrawal and exchange incl. ATM; transfer to another account incl. real-time (RTGS) up to 1 million NIS; voucher payment; clearing-house credits; debits for credit-card payments (excluding the debit card); debits under a standing authorisation or standing order; an instruction to cancel a debit; cheque drawing; cheque deposit (per group of up to 20); cheque encashment; future-dated digital cheque; printing documents from the computerised archive; routine reports; locating documents. Source: Annex F list [BOI]. Relevance: cancelling a standing order through the bank is inside the capped fee.
60. **Cash handling by a teller is separately chargeable only above 100 coins or above 10,000 NIS in notes**, assessed separately for notes and coins; below that it counts as one included action. Source: para 21 [BOI].
61. **Deferred-cheque handling fee only where a physical paper cheque is lodged** at a branch or service box. Source: para 23 [BOI].
62. **Service packages (Part 16)**: banks may sell flexible packages, each approved by the Supervisor; a customer who leaves a package that included account management reverts to the regulated account-management fee; the customer must receive Annex A1 setting out the package contents. Source: paras 18, 19, 32 [BOI].
63. **Who the reform binds**: banking corporations (and payment apps choosing Part 1 or Part 14(א)); it does not set credit-card company fees. Complaints route: the bank, then the BoI Public Enquiries and Consumer Oversight Unit (היחידה לפניות הציבור ולבקרה צרכנית). Source: circular addressee line, para 17; BoI site [BOI].

### H. Telecom switching and number porting

64. **Port, don't cancel**: number portability is the subscriber's right to keep the number when becoming a subscriber of another licensee; the licensee must provide it to every subscriber who asks, within one business day (or shorter if the Minister sets), and without charge. The move is started at the receiving provider. Source: Communications (Telecommunications and Broadcasting) Law s.5א(א), (ו) [VERIFY against Nevo consolidated text; read this round via Hebrew Wikisource only].
65. **Golan Telecom operates under Cellcom's licences since 24.12.2023**, so a Cellcom-to-Golan move is intra-group. Source: golantelecom.co.il/offers [LIVE, already in the skill].
66. **Published budget plan prices and fixed-price periods**: budget carriers (e.g. Rami Levy Mobile) advertise entry plans with a fixed-price period. The skill must tie every quoted price to its fixed-price or promotional period and to s.13א (items 4-10), and must read prices off the carrier's own page rather than quote them from memory. Source: carrier pages [LIVE 2026-10-01].
67. **Telephony is a Third Schedule service, internet and TV are not**: so a mobile promotion may continue under either independent exception: a proper 13א(ד)(1) notice, or the 13א(ד)(2) written-amounts route (including its no-notice case for a discount ending within four months of signing, after which the consumer may leave free of charge). A home-internet or TV promotion has only the default end (13א(ג)(1)) or the 13א(ד)(2) route. Source: Third Schedule, Second Schedule [STAT].

### I. Electricity supplier switching

68. **Same grid, different supplier**: a private supply licensee sells the same electricity at a discount off the regulated IEC tariff; meter, grid and outage handling stay with the network. Source: supplier pages [LIVE]; Electricity Authority supply-competition reform [VERIFY: gov.il pages returned Cloudflare/404 on 2026-10-01].
69. **Track types and meter dependency, as published 2026-10-01**: suppliers offer a flat all-hours discount and time-of-use tracks with larger discounts in set hours (Cellcom Energy: 7.5% flat Sun-Thu, 15% every day 23:00-07:00; its page says smart or basic meters may join). At least one supplier publishes some time-of-use tracks for smart-meter holders only. A time-of-use track saves only if consumption falls in its window; the skill must not quote these rates without re-reading the page. Source: cellcom.co.il energy page and supplier pages [LIVE].
70. **Electricity supply is a Second Schedule business** ("בעל רישיון ספק שירות חיוני" under the Electricity Sector Law 5756-1996), so the s.18ב free-phone and six-minute human-answer duty for cancellation applies; supply is a continuous deal, so ss.14ט and 13ד(ג) govern leaving the supplier. Source: Second Schedule item 6, s.18ב [STAT].

### J. Core saving workflow items the skill already carries (keep, with sourcing)

71. **Subscription audit sources**: card statements, bank standing orders, app-store subscriptions, email. Plus the statutory **annual charges statement each March** for every continuous deal (Jan-Dec charges, total, unpaid balance; burden on the business to prove any debt beyond it), which is the best single audit input. Source: s.13ב(ב1) [STAT].
72. **Stopping a standing authorisation through the bank** as a parallel lever, alongside (not instead of) the 14ט notice that triggers the 31א remedy. Source: s.31א(ב) (remedy keyed to the notice) [STAT]; Annex F "הוראה לביטול חיוב" inside the capped fee [BOI].
73. **Price comparison, cashback platforms and card perks** as live-data activities: rates, card names and caps change, so read the live page; one cashback platform per purchase; PayBack is Max's own service. Source: zap.co.il, cashback.co.il, cashdo.co.il, pay-back.co.il, isracard.co.il [LIVE].
74. **El Al FLY CARD transition**: a CAL-issued FLY CARD keeps earning Matmid points only until 31 December 2026. Source: elal.com FLY CARD FAQ [LIVE, already in skill; re-verify on each update].

## 2. Should cover (advanced)

1. **Displayed price binds at the till** (price on the goods prevails over a higher till price) and 31א(a)(5) exemplary damages if refused. Source: s.17ב(ד), s.31א(א)(5) [STAT].
2. **Special sales (מכירה מיוחדת)**: the business must state what is included, the pre-sale price and the discount or post-discount price; publicised sales must state the minimum number of units. Ties to the "check Zap price history" advice. Source: s.15(א)-(ב1) [STAT].
3. **Total price only, in shekels**, in display and advertising. Source: ss.17ב(ב), 17ד [STAT].
4. **In-store returns policy notice**: a shop must post its policy for non-defect returns; if it posts none, returns are presumed allowed (7 days after a refusal, full refund in the original payment method; opening the package alone is not deterioration); food, perishables, opened media and custom goods excluded. 31א(א)(1)-(2) damages. Source: s.4ג [STAT].
5. **Pre-contract oral and written disclosure in a continuous deal** (right to cancel, end date, cancellation payments and how they are calculated). Source: s.13ג(ב)-(ג) [STAT].
6. **Choice of monthly debit date** among four dates at least six days apart; default the 10th; no surcharge for the resulting delay. Source: s.13ב1 [STAT].
7. **Six-monthly payment breakdown** for usage-based continuous deals and **itemised fixed charges** on the bill (a fixed charge not itemised cannot be collected). Source: s.13ב(א), (ב3) [STAT].
8. **Debt-notice rules before collection** (written separate notice within four months; no collection action for 14 days; no collection costs without it). Relevant when a cancelled subscription produces a "debt". Source: s.13ב2 [STAT].
9. **No payment commission on regulated-tariff services** (and on bills that mix them), and the right to pay by standing authorisation or at Postal Bank. Breach is a 31א(א)(2ג) ground. Whether a private electricity supplier's discounted price counts as a regulated tariff is open [VERIFY]. Source: s.13ח [STAT].
10. **Cancellation for misleading or exploiting distress** within a reasonable time of discovery, refund within seven days. Source: s.32 [STAT].
11. **Overseas tourism distance sales**: the business may offer a choice between the statutory right and the foreign provider's policy, with prior disclosure and burden of proof on the business. Source: s.14ג2 [STAT].
12. **Online bank switching (ניוד חשבון)** as the follow-through when a bank will not cut fees. Source: kolzchut "מעבר בין בנקים באופן מקוון (ניוד)" [title seen in kolzchut search 2026-10-01; body not read, VERIFY].
13. **Mobile exit fees**: whether a private subscriber may be charged for leaving a mobile contract early is set in licence terms and Ministry of Communications decisions, not in the CPL [VERIFY: not found in the Communications Law text read].
14. **Electricity: returning to IEC, switching between private suppliers, and any exit charge**: governed by Electricity Authority supply-licence standards [VERIFY].
15. **Annual Bank of Israel fee statement** (NBT 425) as an audit input; the reform adjusts its package reporting. Source: circular para 35 [BOI].
16. **Housing-loan due-date change fee capped at 5 NIS** exists in the same circular; mention only to route to the mortgage skill. Source: para 27 [BOI].
17. **Residual drafting defect worth flagging**: s.13א(ב)(3) still refers to "פרט 2 לתוספת השנייה" for the voice-call fallback when SMS is blocked, but Second Schedule item 2 is now "(נמחק)". Do not tell users that fallback applies to a particular sector without checking. Source: s.13א(ב)(3), Second Schedule [STAT].
