# Israeli VAT Regulations Summary

## VAT Rate
- Standard rate: **18%** (effective since January 1, 2025; verify the rate in force before computing)
- Previous rate: 17%, in force until 31 December 2024

## Registration Types

### Osek Morsheh (Licensed Dealer)
- Annual turnover above 122,833 NIS (2026 value, indexed every 1 January under VAT Law s.126(a)), or any turnover for a group listed in section 13 of the VAT (Registration) Regulations
- Must charge and collect VAT
- Can claim input VAT deductions
- Must file periodic VAT returns

### Osek Patur (Exempt Dealer)
- Annual turnover up to 122,833 NIS (2026); certain professions and activities must register as osek morsheh regardless of turnover (section 13 of the VAT (Registration) Regulations, https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law01/271_004.htm): confirm status with an accountant
- Files an annual turnover declaration only, by 31 January

## Input VAT Deduction Rules

### Partially Deductible
- Mixed-use costs where the Director set no ratio: 2/3 if use is mainly business, 1/4 if mainly private (reg. 18(b)); vehicle running costs are an illustration only, verify with your accountant

### Non-Deductible
- Buying or importing a private car (reg. 14; exceptions such as driving schools and car rental)
- Entertainment (reg. 16; except entertaining a person from abroad)
- Benefits for employees, owners or managers such as meals, housing and gifts (reg. 15A), unless sold or supplied to them and reported as a taxable transaction
- Buying or building the dealer's own home, even when it is also the place of business (reg. 15)

### Document and Allocation Conditions
- Only a tax invoice issued to the dealer (or an import declaration) supports a deduction, within six months of the document date (VAT Law s.38(a))
- Above NIS 5,000 before VAT (from 1 June 2026) the invoice must carry a number allocated by the Tax Authority (s.38(a1)); earlier invoice dates use the threshold then in force (see SKILL.md Step 5)
