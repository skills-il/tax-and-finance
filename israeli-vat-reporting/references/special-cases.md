# Israeli VAT Special Cases

## Zero-Rated Transactions (0% VAT)
- Some services to tourists (for example lodging, car rental, hospitalisation)
- Services to a foreign resident, except services the Minister of Finance excluded (VAT Law s.30(a)(5); a service is not treated as given to a foreign resident when, under the agreement, it is actually given in Israel to an Israeli resident as well)
- Certain goods set by the Minister of Finance

Important: Zero-rated means VAT at 0%, NOT exempt. The seller does not charge VAT
and can still deduct input VAT on related purchases.

## Exempt Transactions (No VAT)
- The seller charges only the base price, and cannot deduct the input VAT paid to produce that income.
- Exemptions exist only where the law sets them. Verify any claimed exemption against the VAT Law before applying it.

## Eilat
Do not zero-rate a sale or skip VAT because a customer or delivery is in Eilat. Route Eilat cases to a tax adviser or accountant.

## Mixed Transactions and Reverse Charge
- Mixed taxable and exempt activity: route to a tax adviser or accountant.
- Services received from a foreign supplier: route to a tax adviser or accountant; this skill does not compute them.
