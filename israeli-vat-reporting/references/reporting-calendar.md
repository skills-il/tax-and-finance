# Israeli VAT Reporting Calendar

## Monthly Reporters
Any dealer required to file the detailed report (PCN874: turnover above NIS 500,000 or double-entry books; individuals from period 1/2026 unless deferred to 1 January 2027), and otherwise businesses with annual turnover > NIS 1,775,000. Legal form alone does not make a business a monthly reporter.

The table shows the manual (15th) date. Detailed-report filers file and pay by the 23rd; the 19th online date applies only to dealers not required to file the detailed report.

| Period | Manual deadline (non-detailed filers) |
|--------|----------|
| January | February 15 |
| February | March 15 |
| March | April 15 |
| April | May 15 |
| May | June 15 |
| June | July 15 |
| July | August 15 |
| August | September 15 |
| September | October 15 |
| October | November 15 |
| November | December 15 |
| December | January 15 |

## Bi-Monthly Reporters
Businesses with annual turnover < NIS 1,775,000 that are NOT required to file the detailed report.

**Deadline types:**
- Manual filing: 15th of the month after the period ends
- Online filing: 19th of the month after the period ends (by 6:30 PM), only for dealers not required to file the detailed report
- Detailed report filers: file and pay by the 23rd of the month after the period ends

The deadlines below show the manual (15th) date; online filing moves it to the 19th (non-detailed filers only).

| Period | Manual deadline (non-detailed filers) |
|--------|----------|
| January-February | March 15 |
| March-April | May 15 |
| May-June | July 15 |
| July-August | September 15 |
| September-October | November 15 |
| November-December | January 15 |

## Annual Reporters
Osek Patur (exempt dealers) file an annual summary.
- Deadline: 31 January of the following year (declaration of the previous calendar year's turnover)

## Late Filing Penalties
- A late-payment fine (kenas pigurim) and a debt fine (kenas hov)
- Linkage differentials (hatzamada) to CPI plus interest
- An administrative fine under the Administrative Offences Law may also be imposed
- Verify current-year amounts with the Tax Authority
