# Israeli VAT Reporting Calendar

## Monthly Reporters
Any dealer required to file the detailed report (PCN874: turnover above NIS 500,000 or double-entry books; individuals from period 1/2026 unless deferred to 1 January 2027), and otherwise businesses whose turnover in the determining year (the 12 months ending 31 August of the previous year) is above the threshold: NIS 1,775,000 for 2026, NIS 1,805,000 from 1 January 2027 (re-indexed every 1 September). Legal form alone does not make a business a monthly reporter.

The tables show the statutory 15th. The Director moves a 15th, 16th or 23rd that falls on a Friday, Saturday or Sunday to the next business day (usually Monday), and rest days can move it further (see below), so the real date can be later. Remaining 2026 periods per the Tax Authority calendar (manual-filing date, detailed-report date in brackets): September 2026 by 19 October (26 October for detailed filers), October 2026 by 16 November (23 November), November 2026 by 15 December (23 December), December 2026 by 18 January 2027 (26 January 2027).

Detailed-report filers file and pay by the 23rd; the 19th online date applies only to dealers not required to file the detailed report.

## Bi-Monthly Reporters
Businesses whose determining-year turnover is not above the threshold (NIS 1,775,000 for 2026, NIS 1,805,000 from 1 January 2027) and that are NOT required to file the detailed report.

**Deadline types:**
- Manual filing: 15th of the month after the period ends
- Online filing: 19th of the month after the period ends (by 6:30 PM), only for dealers not required to file the detailed report
- Detailed report filers: file and pay by the 23rd of the month after the period ends

The deadlines below show the manual (15th) date; online filing moves it to the 19th (non-detailed filers only).

| Period | Statutory date before any shift (non-detailed filers) |
|--------|----------|
| January-February | March 15 |
| March-April | May 15 |
| May-June | July 15 |
| July-August | September 15 |
| September-October | November 15 (2026: 16 November) |
| November-December | January 15 (for 2026: 18 January 2027) |

## Shifted Deadlines and Nil Returns
- If at least three rest days (Shabbat, holidays, chol hamoed) fall in the five days before a deadline, it moves to the fourth working day after them (VAT Law s.67(b1); s.67A(e) for the detailed report).
- The Tax Authority publishes the actual dates for every period each year. Use that calendar rather than the plain 15th / 19th / 23rd when a date is close to a weekend or holiday.
- A return is due even when the period had no activity, unless the business ceased and the Director was notified (s.67(b)).

## Annual Reporters
Osek Patur (exempt dealers) file an annual summary.
- Deadline: 31 January of the following year (declaration of the previous calendar year's turnover)

## Late Filing and Late Payment
- Filing late: a fixed fine for every two weeks or part of them (VAT Law s.94)
- Paying late: a weekly percentage of the unpaid tax, plus linkage differentials (hatzamada) and interest (s.96, s.97)
- An administrative fine under the Administrative Offences Law may also be imposed
- Verify current amounts with the Tax Authority
