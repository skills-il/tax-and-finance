# Domain checklist: israeli-client-payment-chaser

Coverage contract for this skill, reviewed 2026-10-29. Each row names a topic, why a practitioner treats it as core, and where the skill covers it. The rules themselves, with their citations, live in SKILL.md and references/legal-escalation.md, backed by evidence.json; this file states no rule of its own.
