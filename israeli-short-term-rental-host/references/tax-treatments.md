# Tax treatments for short-term rental income

Not tax advice. Every figure is per tax year; use the year the income belongs to. The base for
every route is what the guest paid (nightly price plus cleaning fee charged); the platform's fee is
an expense, not a reduction of receipts.

## The four treatments

| Treatment | What it costs | Conditions | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business income | Profit (after deductible expenses) at the host's marginal rate, plus National Insurance on the profit; annual return | The Tax Authority's reported position for regular short-term renting | ynet 2016, Globes 2017 (statements) |
| Section 122 | 10% of gross rent; no expenses, no depreciation; paid within 30 days of year end | Residential flat, and the income is NOT business income | Income Tax Ordinance s.122 |
| Exemption law, 1990 | Up to the monthly ceiling tax-free; above it the exempt amount is the ceiling minus the excess; zero at twice the ceiling; the rest taxed | Flat used as a residence by an individual tenant; not a business-books flat; a document signed by the tenant confirming residential use only | Exemption law s.1-3 |
| Regular rent route | Profit at the marginal rate, from 31% under age 60, lower brackets from age 60 | Rent that is neither a business nor in a special track | Kol Zchut, rental tax routes |

## Deductible expenses

- Running costs of a home the host also lives in are personal expenses (Ordinance s.32(1)); only
  the rental share is deductible, by nights rented or by area.
- The platform fee, cleaning, laundry and supplies per booking are expenses of the activity.
- Under section 122 nothing is deductible.

## Exemption ceiling by year (NIS per month, all the household's residential rent combined)

| Tax year | Ceiling | Exemption reaches zero at |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 5,654 | 11,308 |
| 2025 | 5,654 | 11,308 |
| 2026 | 5,654 | 11,308 |
| 2027 | 5,654 | 11,308 |

The statute freezes the ceiling at 5,654 for 2024 to 2027. Rent is counted in the month of the
nights it pays for.

## VAT

| Item | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| VAT rate | 18% from 1 January 2025 | Government decision 1270 |
| Osek patur ceiling | 120,000 NIS (2024, 2025); 122,833 NIS (2026) | VAT Law s.1 |
| Registration | a dealer must register (s.52) | VAT Law |
| Residential rental exemption | s.31(1): residential rental up to 25 years, except lodging in a hotel | VAT Law |
| Hotel | a place that usually lodges at least five people for payment | VAT Law s.1 |
| Tourist zero rate | lodging of a tourist in a hotel | VAT Law s.30 |

The Tax Authority's 2017 statement (Globes) said that renting a flat to a tourist is generally
VAT-liable at a zero rate. The statute's zero rate names lodging in a hotel. Plan on registering,
and settle with a professional which stays are zero-rated.

## The Tax Authority's position, as reported

- ynet, 28.06.2016, on an audit of hosts who rent short-term on a regular basis: "ופעולה זו משמשת
  להם כפעילות עסקית לכל דבר". The same statement says one-off rentals were not the target.
- Globes, 2017, the spokesperson: short-term rental is like renting a zimmer or any other business,
  "ולא ניתן לקבל בגינה את ההטבות הקבועות בחוק להשכרת דירה למגורים".
- No circular, ruling or FAQ on short-term rental was found on gov.il as of November 2026.

## Unresolved (send to a tax adviser or accountant)

- A room in the host's own home.
- A one-off rental while the family is abroad.
- Whether offering no services changes the classification.
- Which stays are zero-rated for VAT.

## If the activity is a business

- Income-tax file and VAT registration before starting (VAT Law s.52).
- Advance payments during the year (Ordinance s.175).
- Annual return and books, with a receipt for every booking.
- National Insurance registration if the self-employed definition is met.

## Reporting rent

- Annual-return filers report residential rent in the return's rent fields (directive 5/2026).
- Directive 5/2026 runs online reporting for those paying the 10% without a return; from tax year
  2024 a lease or a lawyer-signed affidavit is attached to an online rental report.
- Income from the same flat cannot be split between routes in the same year (Kol Zchut).

## National Insurance

- Not counted for contributions: rent taxed under s.122 and tax-exempt non-work income (s.350).
  Rent taxed on the regular route is not covered by that exemption.
- Self-employed status turns on hours (at least 20 a week on average) or income relative to the
  average wage; check current figures on btl.gov.il.

## Selling the flat

- Section 122(c): after using the 10% route, betterment tax on a sale adds back the depreciation
  that could have been claimed for those years.
