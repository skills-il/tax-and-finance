# Host checklist and guest texts

## Before the first guest

- [ ] Building: read the registered bylaws (תקנון). A condominium registrar has barred short-term
      rental where the bylaws limit use to residence.
- [ ] Planning: a 2025 Tel Aviv magistrate ruling held rentals of less than a month to be a
      non-conforming use of a residential flat. Treat it as a risk.
- [ ] Lease (if the host is a tenant): subletting must be allowed in writing.
- [ ] Insurance: notify the home insurer in writing of the material change (paying guests) and keep the answer.
- [ ] Licence: more than four holiday units with services needs a business licence.
- [ ] Records: keep the platform's earnings export, invoices for cleaning and supplies, utility and arnona bills.
- [ ] VAT: compare expected annual payouts with the osek patur ceiling; get a professional view on the VAT reading.
- [ ] Safety: smoke detector, fire blanket, first-aid kit; know where the protected space is.

## Listing template (fill from the host's facts only)

Hebrew and English versions side by side. Sections: what is rented, sleeping arrangements, kitchen
and bathroom, access (stairs, lift), parking, neighbourhood, what is not included. Do not write
"licensed", "kosher" or "accessible" unless the host states it and can back it.

## House rules template

- Maximum guests, no events.
- Quiet hours matching the building's bylaws.
- Smoking rules.
- Waste and recycling instructions for the building.
- Check-in and check-out times, key handover.

## Safety card for guests (Hebrew and English)

- Where the protected space (ממ"ד) or nearest shelter is, and to go there when a siren sounds.
- Emergency numbers for police, ambulance and fire (the israeli-emergency-guide skill lists them).
- Where the gas and main water shut-offs are.
