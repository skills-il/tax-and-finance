# Comparing bank offers and exiting early: Directive 451 and the early-repayment order

Directive 329 (see SKILL.md Step 2) limits what a bank may LEND. The two sources below govern
what a bank must SHOW the borrower and what it may CHARGE on early repayment. Read this file
before comparing two approvals in principle or pricing a refinance.

Sources:
- Proper Conduct of Banking Business Directive 451, "Procedures for Extending Housing Loans"
  (נהלים למתן הלוואות לדיור), version [22] (7/23): https://www.boi.org.il/media/utld2tgp/451.pdf
- Banking Order (Early Repayment of a Housing Loan), 2002 (צו הבנקאות (פירעון מוקדם של הלוואה
  לדיור), התשס"ב-2002), Bank of Israel consolidated text: https://www.boi.org.il/media/qy5cow0l/116.pdf

## 1. The approval in principle (אישור עקרוני), Directive 451 section 4c

- The bank must answer a loan request **in writing within 5 business days** (7 in exceptional
  cases defined in advance in its credit policy, with notice of the delay and the reason for it).
  The directive requires an answer, not a reason for a refusal. Do not tell a user the bank is
  obliged to explain a rejection; tell them to ask.
- The terms in the approval hold for a period the bank sets, which **may not be less than 24 days**
  from the date of the approval and must be stated prominently on it. Two offers from two banks
  therefore expire; a negotiation that runs past the stated period can lose the quoted rates.
- The approval is issued at a stage where no costs are charged to the applicant.

### The uniform baskets (הסלים האחידים), Appendix 5

Every approval must show, next to the bank's own proposed mix (סל מוצע), three uniform baskets
priced by that bank. They exist so that two banks can be compared on identical mixes:

| Basket | Composition |
|---|---|
| Uniform basket 1 | 100% fixed non-linked |
| Uniform basket 2 | 1/3 fixed non-linked, 1/3 Prime, 1/3 variable CPI-linked resetting every 5 years on a government-bond base |
| Uniform basket 3 | 1/2 fixed non-linked, 1/2 Prime |

In the uniform baskets the repayment method is Spitzer, and the borrower chooses one term from
10, 15, 20, 25 or 30 years. Uniform baskets are not required for some loan types, including
any-purpose loans up to 120,000 NIS or under 10 years, and bridge loans of up to three years.

**How to use this when comparing:** compare basket 1 against basket 1, basket 2 against basket 2,
and basket 3 against basket 3 across the banks FIRST, on the same term. Only then compare the
proposed mixes, which differ by design. Custom mixes built by the agent (SKILL.md Step 4) are a
supplement, not a replacement, for this like-for-like comparison.

### Figures the approval already prints

Each basket shows the total projected interest (including fees and projections), the first
monthly payment, the highest monthly payment expected under the projection, and the total amount
expected to be repaid over the loan. Read these off the documents first and recompute only where
the documents do not cover the question (for example a custom mix, or a different inflation
scenario). The projections are the bank's; treat them as scenarios, not forecasts.

## 2. Insurance and appraisal, Directive 451 sections 9 to 11

- The bank may require property insurance and life insurance up to the loan amount, but it must
  tell the borrower that both may be bought **directly, not through an insurance agency the bank
  controls** (external insurance), and it may only insist on the conditions needed to protect its
  rights.
- No insurance may be required on loans of up to **30,000 NIS**, or once the outstanding balance
  falls to that amount.
- Appraisal cost: where the bank sends the appraiser, it may charge the lower of its actual cost
  and its published fee schedule, and must give the borrower a copy of the report. A bank may not
  unreasonably refuse an appraisal made for another bank for a housing loan, where the appraiser is
  on the list given with the approval and **no more than 90 days** have passed since it was made.

## 3. Mortgage porting (גרירת משכנתא), Directive 451 section 20

A bank may not refuse a borrower's request to substitute another property as collateral for the
existing loan, and may not change the loan's terms, except for reasonable cause, and it must tell
the borrower the reason. Relevant when the user is selling one home and buying another rather than
refinancing.

## 4. Early-repayment fees, the 2002 Order

**A directed (eligibility) loan carries no early-repayment fee at all (section 2).** On any other
housing loan, a bank may condition early repayment on these fees and no others:

| Fee | Rule | Section |
|---|---|---|
| Operational fee | Up to 60 NIS as stated in the order (the Supervisor may update the amount by the index) | 3(1), 9(b) |
| Short-notice fee | One tenth of a percent of the amount repaid, if the borrower gave under ten days' notice. Not charged on the part of a new loan the same bank gives for the repayment, or on the borrower's death, and not on a rate-change date (4(3)). Notice may not be given more than 45 days ahead | 3(2), 10(b) |
| Capitalisation fee (עמלת היוון) | Computation A (3(3)): only where the Supervisor's published **average rate** today is below the loan's rate, the present-value difference of the future payments at the two rates. Computation B (3(4)): where the average rate at ORIGINATION was below the loan's rate, the present-value difference between today's average rate and the origination average rate. The bank charges the **lower** of A and B (3(5)); B is zero when today's average rate is at or above the origination average rate. The benchmark is the Supervisor's average rate, not a bank's current quote | 3(3), 3(4), 3(5) |
| CPI-average fee (CPI-linked loans) | If repaid between the 1st and the 15th of the month: the amount repaid times half the average CPI change over the last twelve published indices. Repaying after the 15th avoids it | 5 |

**Variable tracks, Prime included.** Where the rate-change dates are not known in advance, or are
set at once a year or more often, **no capitalisation fee** is charged (section 4(2)). A repayment
made on a rate-change date carries the operational fee only (section 4(3)). So "Prime has no
penalty" is right about the capitalisation fee, but the operational fee and the short-notice fee
can still apply. A 5-year variable track repaid between reset dates can carry a capitalisation
fee.

**When the average rate is ABOVE the loan's rate** at repayment, the negative capitalisation value
is offset against the other fees (section 7(b)).

**Discounts on the capitalisation fee by elapsed time (section 8), measured from each loan's exact
execution date, not a rounded "five years ago":**

| Loan type | Discount |
|---|---|
| Ordinary loan (8(b)) | 20% from the end of year 3 to before the end of year 5; 30% from the end of year 5 |
| Complementary loan (8(a)): a bank loan given to a person entitled to a directed loan, in addition to it and for the same purpose, whenever it was given | 10% from the end of year 1, 20% from year 2, 30% from year 3, 40% from year 4 |

Factor these into any refinancing break-even, including Example 2 in SKILL.md: if that borrower
also took a directed loan, the bank loan given alongside it is complementary and the discount at
five years is 40%, not 30%.

**Partial early repayment** shortens the loan term by default under Directive 451 section 15(a),
though the borrower may ask otherwise. State the assumption in any calculation.

## 5. What "property value" means for LTV

The LTV denominator is not the purchase price. The Bank of Israel's Q&A on Directive 329 quotes the
reporting-directive definition: the property value "shall not exceed the lower of the appraiser's
valuation and the cost of the property in the purchase agreement" (or the expected cost for a
property under construction or a purchase group). If the appraisal comes in below the price, the
maximum loan shrinks and the buyer must find more equity. Source (current Q&A file, February 2026):
https://www.boi.org.il/media/emunmobp/202601.pdf

This is also why the approval in principle and, where possible, the appraisal should come BEFORE
the purchase agreement is signed.
